== Overview

Defect archetypes, real-corpus fixtures and verification hints that guard the specifications of
link:Specification.adoc[this repository] during implementation. The item format and the lifecycle of an item are those
of the link:../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/ImplementationWatch.adoc[Implementation Watch] of the documentation;
the archetypes that span all specifications are in its
link:../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/implementation-watch/cross-cutting.adoc[`cross-cutting.adoc`].

The watch items stay with the specification section they watch and keep their identifiers: an item whose section moved
here moved here with it, and an item whose section stayed behind stays with it. No identifier is renumbered.

== Items Whose Section Stayed Behind

The watch items of the same area codes that watch a section which is not held here keep their place with it:
`PM-WATCH-CLI-15` to `PM-WATCH-CLI-21`, `PM-WATCH-CLI-26` and `PM-WATCH-CLI-27` (web access and pairing),
`PM-WATCH-RUN-1`, `-4`, `-5`, `-7`, `-12`, `-14`, `-17`, `-18` (the runtime) and `PM-WATCH-RUN-3` (verification). They
are not repeated here, and their identifiers are not reused.
