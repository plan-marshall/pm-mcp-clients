link:../cli/README.adoc[CLI Review] » Verification Results » The Terminal Acts

This document records the eleven checks of the terminal client's acting, answering, notices, onboarding, cleanup, and
system model against the merged code. The checks come from
link:../cli/use-cases/verification.adoc[CLI Review, Use Cases, Verification] (`CLI-UC-V-01` to `CLI-UC-V-10`) and
link:../cli/cross-cutting/verification.adoc[CLI Review, Cross-Cutting, Verification] (`CLI-CC-V-06`). Each check was
executed and each row names what ran: the screens cover `en`/`de` at 120 × 30 and 80 × 24, and the test runs are the
green `mvn` runs of the check's own test names. Two checks are `not verified` with their reason: `CLI-UC-V-05`, whose
typed-text clause no harness can evaluate (it sends a broadcast before it types), and `CLI-UC-V-10`, whose operator
agreement is not a check a plan can run. Four checks carry a `fail`, each routed as one finding to the epic inbox.
Nothing that was found was fixed.

Where a check's sample wording and the screen differ, this document applies one rule, stated here once: a sample's exact
spelling is illustrative (the key range `1-2` against the three-option `1-3`, the badge `pm [3 questions] ›` against the
segmented badge), while a named verb, a named string or a required clause that does not hold is behavioural and fails
(`onboard --auto --yes`, `No plan nope`, the German language clause, the waiver's missing cases). The Amendment
predicted `CLI-UC-V-01`'s "each option shows its consequence" as a `fail`; the capture shows all three consequences, so
the prediction does not hold and the check passes.
