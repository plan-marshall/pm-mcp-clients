[#CC-F-01]
== CC-F-01: German and English mix on one screen

* Severity: `blocker` (for a product)
* Seen in `de`:
** plan tree: "phase init fertig" beside "TASK-001 … done"; head "execute running 2 h 10 min";
** off-track banner: "vom Weg ab: repeating (5 von 5): show";
** system: "1 waiting for_you", "3 running", "2 paused", "busy", "idle", "1 retry", "ok";
** overview: "8 d ago", "failed", "running"; ended work: "Grund: the edit was not ours";
** machine settings: "Deutsch, models English", "4 models on 2 harnesses", "31 roles", "SonarCloud not available";
** epic: "2 documents", "1 issue"; events "relay-auth-v2 asks a decision";
** completion report: "MERGED", "done".
* Seen in `en` after a switch from `de`: "3 Pläne · 1 Epic", "die Einrichtung braucht dich"; and back in `de`: "3 plans
  · 1 epic", "setup needs you".
* Note: some English is model-written content (decision texts, reasons). That is correct: the model language may differ
  (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/articles/karpathy-ste100-output.adoc[ASD-STE100 Output]). The
  defect is the interface's own words.

[#CC-F-03]
== CC-F-03: Placeholders in titles

* Severity: `major`
* Seen: browser tab titles "(3) {plan}", "(3) {epic}", "Einen Plan in {project} starten".
* Cause: `routes/+layout.svelte` calls `$t(ROUTE_TITLE_KEYS[route])` without arguments; the keys `plan.view.title`,
  `epic.view.title`, `start.plan.title` carry parameters.

[#CC-F-06]
== CC-F-06: The theme is not remembered

* Severity: `minor`
* Seen: light theme chosen; after a navigation with a full load, the app is dark again. The language is remembered.
* Cause: `toggleTheme()` in `routes/+layout.svelte` only sets `data-theme`; start-up reads `prefers-color-scheme`.
* Breaks: link:../../design/web-app/README.adoc#themes[Light and Dark Themes] ("remembered per browser").
