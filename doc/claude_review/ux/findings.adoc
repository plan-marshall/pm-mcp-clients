[#keep]
== Patterns to Keep

* *Tree and narrative* (plan view): the structure on the left, the story of the selected part on the right. Extend it
  to the epic (workstreams left, plan narrative right) and to the designer (structure left, text right).
* *Consent screens* (onboarding): what, where, what runs, what never happens, the tokens; then the three answers.
* *Labels that name the act*: "Check 2 harnesses, Claude Code the default", "Delete 8 items…", "Pause the plan".
* *Inline choice for a small act*: the pause panel with "where it holds".
* *The completion report and the cleanup preview*: structured, scannable, with the act at the end.

== Findings

[#UX-F-01]
=== UX-F-01: No single place for "what needs me"

* Severity: `blocker` (for a production tool)
* Seen: attention is spread over five places:
** the sidebar of the overview ("!2", "!1", with no word on what);
** the header counts (decisions 3, reports 5);
** the block "Nebenher" at the bottom of one project ("! Landen fehlgeschlagen");
** rows inside an epic ("wartet auf dich");
** the project cards ("die Einrichtung braucht dich").
* Effect: task T1 takes four screens and still misses holds and expiring tokens
  (link:analysis.adoc#tasks[Analysis, Key Tasks]).

[#UX-F-02]
=== UX-F-02: The navigation is flat, and has no jump by name

* Seen: seven header items of equal weight, plus language, theme, and the machine badge. Daily items sit beside rare
  items. At 390 px the header takes two rows (about a quarter of the screen); the designer, system, and settings
  items become emoji without a label (✎, 🖥, ⚙).
* Missing: a search or command palette to go to a plan, an epic, a decision, or a pull request by name.

[#UX-F-03]
=== UX-F-03: Settings live on two pages with near-identical names

* Seen: "Einstellungen der Maschine" (`/settings/machine`) and "Maschinen-Einstellungen" (`/settings`). Each has a
  button to the other. The header item opens the first; paired devices hide behind "Verwalte sie" at its bottom.
* See also link:../use-cases/findings-onboarding-entry.adoc#UC-F-11[UC-F-11].

[#UX-F-04]
=== UX-F-04: No primary act per state

* Seen:
** Epic: nine buttons of the same style, including "Archiviere das Epic" and "Brich das Epic ab".
** Plan head: "Prozess gestalten", "Spring dorthin, wo er steht", "#214", "Zeige die Details", "Zurück": a button, a
   button, a pill link, an underlined link, plain text.
** Plan acts: "Halte den Plan an", "Brich den Plan ab", "Chat", "Einstellungen", the same style.
* Effect: the operator must read every label to find what the state asks.

[#UX-F-05]
=== UX-F-05: Destructive acts are at rest and dominant

* Seen:
** Project cards: "Freigeben…" and "Austragen…" in red on every card.
** Paired devices: four filled red buttons, one per device, plus "revoke all others".
** Project onboarding: "Verwirf es" is the strongest button; "Setze fort" is secondary.
* Expected: destructive acts are rare. They belong in an overflow menu, and they become red only in their
  confirmation.

[#UX-F-06]
=== UX-F-06: Paths end in raw error codes

* Seen:
** "Zeige die Details" on a running plan → `plan_not_found` and "Erneut versuchen".
** "Start the plan" with a project unknown to the engine → `project_not_found` in red, nothing else.
* Expected: what happened, why, and what to do, with an act.

[#UX-F-07]
=== UX-F-07: A control of another view sits in the plan head

* Seen: "Zeige die Details" in the plan head is the report control ("Öffne die Details des Berichts…"). For a running
  plan, it leads to an error. See link:../use-cases/findings-plans-control.adoc#UC-F-18[UC-F-18].

[#UX-F-08]
=== UX-F-08: The plan tree promises more than it has

* Seen: every finished phase has an expand control, and expanding shows nothing. Selecting it shows "Dieser Teil hat
  keine Aktivität auf Task-Ebene." The token line offers a breakdown that "is not reported yet". The labels repeat
  the kind: "PHASE phase init", "TASKS execute.envelope", "SUB_WORKFLOW sub-workflow remediation.ci-failure".
* Effect: the operator learns that clicking in the tree is often useless.

[#UX-F-09]
=== UX-F-09: Ids where titles belong

* Seen: the showroom plan head shows `drop-legacy-api` where the overview says "Drop the legacy endpoints". The epic's
  workstreams list plan ids. The cleanup lists ids. The breadcrumb mixes "Relay Hardening" (title) and
  `relay-hardening` (id).
* Expected: the title first; the id small, beside it, copyable.

[#UX-F-10]
=== UX-F-10: Every view has its own layout

* Seen: content width about 460 px (projects), 670 px (decisions), 730 px (reports), 960 px (system), full width
  (overview, plan, epic, designer). Page heads differ: a title with buttons, a title with a pill, a breadcrumb with five
  controls, an eyebrow "Quellen" above a title.
* Effect: on a 1566 px screen the projects page uses less than a third of the width; the operator's eye jumps between
  layouts.

[#UX-F-11]
=== UX-F-11: Decisions are a long list of full cards

* Seen: eight open decisions in `running-work`, each a full card with evidence, options, a note field, and four
  buttons. No order, no grouping, no compact triage row. The layouts "Kompakt" and "Geteilt" exist but are not the
  default.
* Expected: a triage list (what, plan, blocks, since) with the detail beside it.

[#UX-F-12]
=== UX-F-12: The start flow looks unfinished

* Seen: plain text buttons, two buttons "Beschreibung" and "+ Beschreibung", an orphan heading "Chat", no step
  indicator, no back, no styled primary act. Details:
  link:../use-cases/findings-projects-starting.adoc#UC-F-34[UC-F-34].

image::{shots}/vd-start-choice.jpg[The first start screen: a title and two plain text buttons,width=640]

[#UX-F-13]
=== UX-F-13: The overview rows do not scan

* Seen: the word "Aktionen" floats in the middle of each row (it is a menu button without the look of one). State,
  phase, and "needs you" sit at the right edge in pills of different colours. The block "Nebenher" with a failed
  landing sits below the plans, where the eye arrives last. The ended-work table collapses its columns ("Pull
  RequestBeendet").
* Expected: aligned columns (title, state, phase, since, progress, needs you); the row menu at the right as an icon
  button; failures in the attention inbox.

[#UX-F-14]
=== UX-F-14: Loading has no form

* Seen: the bare word "checking" (start); "Die gekoppelten Geräte werden geladen …" on the machine settings page,
  which loads more than devices; a spinner alone (cleanup, 4 s); the header badges appear late.
* Expected: skeletons in the shape of the content; a time limit; a cause when it fails.

image::{shots}/ux-machine-settings-loading.jpg[The machine settings page announces paired devices while it loads everything,width=640]

[#UX-F-15]
=== UX-F-15: Designer results are out of sight

* Seen: "Validate" writes its results into a thin strip at the bottom edge. The installed units list truncates its
  acts ("Entferne…", "Entfe") and scrolls sideways. The head has "Zurück zum Designer" while the designer is open.
* Expected: results above the text, linked to lines; the units list readable at its width.

image::{shots}/ux-designer-validate.jpg[The designer after Validate: results in a strip at the bottom edge,width=640]

[#UX-F-16]
=== UX-F-16: Forms save per field

* Seen: plan settings with nine "Save" buttons; machine settings with "Speichern" per block and "Übernimm diese
  Fristen" for two numbers.
* Expected: one form, one save, a clear "changed" mark, and "reset to inherited" per field.

[#UX-F-17]
=== UX-F-17: Live state is invisible

* Seen: the app receives events (SSE), but nothing shows that the view is live or when it was last updated. The system
  view has a manual refresh button beside live data.
* Expected: a quiet live indicator in the shell; "stream lost, reconnecting" when it is lost (UC-SYS-2 asks for it).
