_Use cases: link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#UC-PLN-4[UC-PLN-4], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#UC-PLN-5[UC-PLN-5]_

What the overview of running work shows and in which order is link:../shared/plan-overview.adoc[Plan Overview], for both clients (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#UC-PLN-4[UC-PLN-4]). This part is how the console draws it: as a full-screen view for navigating, and as a tree in the scrollback for reading. The conventions of the transcripts are those of xref:05-transcripts-entry.adoc#transcripts[Transcripts].

[#overview-ways]
== Two Ways

* *`plans`* opens the full-screen view: two levels to move through with the keyboard, live, until the operator leaves it (<<full-screen>>).
* *`status`* prints the same content once as a tree into the scrollback: to read, to copy, for a screen reader, and on every terminal the full-screen view cannot use (<<status-tree>>). It is what `plans` prints instead where the full-screen view is not available.
* The prompt's badges keep counting what waits while neither is open (xref:04-console.adoc#prompt[The Prompt]).

[#full-screen]
== The Full-Screen View

[#full-screen-layout]
=== Layout

----
 Running work · projects                                          ● connected
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ! 2  pm-mcp-clients      3 plans · 1 epic                    2 min ago
▶     pm-mcp-core         2 plans                                     9 min ago
      plan-marshall-mcp   1 epic                              1 h ago
 ! 1  plan-marshall       setup needs you
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ↑↓ move   Enter open   E ended work   R 5 reports   / filter   ? help   q leave
----

* *Header*: where the operator is (`projects`, or the project and its view) and the connection to the runtime.
* *List*: one row per project, plan, or epic, the selected one marked `▶` and drawn inverted; it scrolls when it is longer than the screen, with `↑ 3 more` and `↓ 5 more` at its edges.
* *Footer*: the keys that apply here; `?` shows all of them.
* The view opens on the terminal's alternate screen, so the scrollback and the prompt are as they were when it is left.

[#full-screen-project]
=== A Project

By plan:

----
 Running work · pm-mcp-clients · by plan                          ● connected
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ! decision  ▸ plan  Console history of answers       refine · waiting for you
                     running 40 min · in refine 12 min · 0 of 0 tasks
▶            ▸ plan  Overview resource of the runtime execute · running
                     running 2 h 10 min · in execute 25 min · 7 of 12 tasks
                     in ◇ client work
             ▸ plan  Message catalogue                outline · pausing after the task
                     running 1 h 5 min · in outline 30 min · 0 of 0 tasks
                     in ◇ client work
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ↑↓ move   Enter open   a actions   e epic   ! what waits   Tab by epic   E ended work   Esc back
----

By epic:

----
 Running work · pm-mcp-clients · by epic                  ● connected
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
▶            ◇ epic  client work          2 running · 3 staged
                     running 3 d · 4 of 9 plans
               ├─    ▸ plan  Overview resource of the runtime  execute · running
               └─    ▸ plan  Message catalogue                 outline · pausing after the task
   without epic
 ! decision  ▸ plan  Console history of answers       refine · waiting for you
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ↑↓ move   Enter open   a actions   ! what waits   Tab by plan   E ended work   Esc back
----

* Each row is the plan's or epic's first line (kind, title, phase and state) and below it its details (times, progress, the epic it belongs to). On a terminal narrower than 100 columns the details line is left out and `?` on the row shows it; below 60 columns the view does not open and `status` prints instead.
* *The state* of a plan is one of a closed list, the texts `overview.state.*` of link:../shared/plan-overview.adoc#texts[Plan Overview, Texts]: `running`, `pausing` (with its point: `after the task`, `after the phase`), `paused`, `waiting for you`, `held`. A plan's own pull request, where it has one, is shown in its details by its number (`#214`), a terminal hyperlink (OSC 8) to the pull request where the terminal shows one; `?` on the row prints its URL.
* *The marker* `!` names the kind of the first item that needs the operator, the texts `overview.needs.*`: `decision`, `waiver`, `waits for you` (an operator wait), the holds `stalled`, `needs a fix` (a condition), `integrity pause`, `held`, `landing failed`, and `new findings`, `setup`. A paused plan carries no marker for being paused: its state says it.
* `e` on a plan moves to its epic in the view by epic; `Tab` switches between the two views and keeps the selection.
* `Enter` on a plan or an epic opens its own view in the same full-screen view: a plan its plan view (xref:16-plan-view.adoc[The Plan View]); an epic its epic view (link:../shared/epic-view.adoc[Epic View]; drawn as xref:19-actions.adoc#epic-view[An Epic's View] says); `!` leaves the view and opens what waits for the operator in it, as `answer` does (link:../shared/decisions.adoc[Decisions]).
* `a` opens the actions of the selected plan or epic below its row (xref:19-actions.adoc#full-screen[Actions]); `E` opens the project's ended work (<<ended>>).

[#full-screen-keys]
=== Keys

The keys follow the conventions of `k9s`, `lazygit`, and `tig`; they are the same in every language, because they are keys of navigation, not mnemonics of a label.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Key |Action

|`↑` `↓`, `k` `j`
|move the selection

|`PgUp` `PgDn`, `g` `G`
|a page; the first and the last row

|`Enter`, `→`, `l`
|open: a project shows its plans; a plan or an epic opens its own view

|`Esc`, `←`, `h`
|back one level

|`Tab`
|switch the view of a project: by plan, by epic

|`e`
|on a plan: its epic

|`a`
|the actions of the selected plan or epic, below its row (xref:19-actions.adoc#full-screen[Actions])

|`!`
|open what waits for the operator in the selected row

|`E`
|the ended work of the selected project, or of the project shown (<<ended>>)

|`S`
|the system view, as a level of the view (xref:25-system.adoc#full-screen[System, Full Screen])

|`R`
|the unread reports, as a level of the view; the footer of the projects' level shows their count while there are any (xref:24-reports.adoc#full-screen[Reports, In the Full-Screen View])

|`/`
|filter the rows by a part of their name or title; `Esc` clears it

|`r`
|read everything again from the runtime

|`?`
|all keys, and the details of the selected row

|`q`
|leave the view to the prompt
|===

[#full-screen-live]
=== Live, and Leaving

* The view follows the runtime's events and redraws only what changed; the selection stays on the selected item, wherever its row moves. A notice that would be printed above the prompt is held while the view is open.
* *Leaving* (`q`) restores the screen the operator left and prints one line of what arrived meanwhile, then the prompt:

[source,text]
----
pm › plans
  (the full-screen view, until q)
While the overview was open: 1 decision arrived (Q-014, plan fix-relay), 2 new reports (reports).
pm [1 question] ›
----

[#full-screen-build]
=== How It Is Built

* On JLine 4 alone, without another library: `Display` draws the screen and updates only the cells that changed, `BindingReader` and `KeyMap` read the keys, the terminal's capabilities switch to the alternate screen, hide the cursor, and set the keypad mode; everything is restored in a `finally`, also on an error, and the terminal's resize signal redraws. JLine's own `ttop` is built the same way.
* The view model (the rows, the selection by id, the filter, the actions of the keys) is kept apart from the drawing, so that the drawing can change its library later without the model.
* One thread draws: the events of the runtime only update the model and mark it changed, and the view's loop redraws, waiting for a key with a short timeout.
* `console.full_screen` of `machine-config.json` switches the full-screen view off (`off`) or leaves it on where the terminal allows it (`auto`, the default); off, `plans` prints the tree as `status` does.

[#overview-persistence]
=== Where the Operator Left It

The view stores the project, the view of the project, and the id of the selected plan or epic in `$XDG_STATE_HOME/pm-operator/overview.json` (default `~/.local/state/pm-operator/overview.json`), when the operator leaves it; the next `plans` opens there, or falls back as link:../shared/plan-overview.adoc#persistence[Where the Operator Left It] says. Like the history, the file lies outside `<PM_MCP_BASE>` and holds no content of a plan.

[#status-tree]
== The Tree (`status`)

[source,text]
----
pm › status
Runtime pm-mcpd 0.9.1, running since 08:14 · 2 workers · 1 of 2 build slots in use

! 2  pm-mcp-clients · 3 plans · 1 epic
     ! decision  plan  Console history of answers   refine · waiting for you · 40 min
                 plan  Overview resource of the runtime   execute · running · 2 h 10 min
                       in epic client work · 7 of 12 tasks
                 plan  Message catalogue   outline · pausing after the task · 1 h 5 min
                       in epic client work
     pm-mcp-core · 2 plans
                 plan  Session log and generations   execute · running · 4 h
                       pull request #198 https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/pull/198
                 plan  Profile loader   finalize · paused · 6 h
! 1  plan-marshall · setup needs you
pm [1 question] ›
----

* The same rows, in the same order, as words: the kind is a word (`plan`, `epic`), the state is a word of the closed list (`running`, `pausing`, `paused`, `waiting for you`, `held`), the marker `!` is followed by its kind, and no meaning is in a colour or a glyph alone.
* A plan's own pull request is printed by its number and its URL, so that it can be opened from the scrollback.
* By default the tree is the view by plan: plans flat, each naming its epic. `status --by epic` prints the view by epic: the epics with their running and staged plans, and their plans below them; `status --project <id>` prints one project; `status --ended` prints the ended work (<<ended>>).
* The tree is printed once; it is the view of a screen reader, of a terminal without cursor control, and of a narrow one.

[#ended]
== Ended Work

What ended work lists is link:../shared/plan-overview.adoc#ended[Plan Overview, Ended Work] (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#UC-PLN-4[UC-PLN-4], its variation). The terminal reaches it two ways:

* *`E`* in the full-screen view opens the ended work of the selected project (on the level of the projects) or of the project shown, as a level of the same view; `Esc` goes back to where it was opened.
* *`status --ended [--project <project_id>]`* prints it into the scrollback; without `--project`, the project of the working directory, else every project with ended work, newest first.

----
 Ended work · pm-mcp-clients                                      ● connected
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
▶ ▸ plan  Relay drops events on reconnect   merged      #211 · 2 d ago
  ▸ plan  Fix the relay's event loss        cancelled   2 d ago
            the approach is replaced by plan relay-v2
  ◇ epic  client bootstrap                  completed   2 weeks ago
  ▸ plan  Message catalogue v0              abandoned   3 weeks ago
            superseded by the shared texts
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ↑↓ move   Enter open   a actions   / filter   Esc back
----

[source,text]
----
pm › status --ended --project pm-mcp-clients
pm-mcp-clients · ended work
  plan  Relay drops events on reconnect   merged · 2 d ago
        pull request #211 https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/pull/211
  plan  Fix the relay's event loss   cancelled · 2 d ago
        reason: the approach is replaced by plan relay-v2
  epic  client bootstrap   completed · 2 weeks ago
  plan  Message catalogue v0   abandoned · 3 weeks ago
        reason: superseded by the shared texts
pm ›
----

* Each row is the plan or epic with its outcome (a plan: `merged`, `no change`, `handed back`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released`, `aborted`; an epic: `completed`, `cancelled`, `released`; link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#outcomes[How a Plan Ends]), its reason where it has one, and when it ended; newest first; the client reads the runtime's pages as the operator scrolls (full screen) or as it prints (`status --ended`).
* `Enter` on an ended plan opens its plan view as it ended (xref:16-plan-view.adoc#ended[An Ended Plan]), on an ended epic its epic view as it ended (link:../shared/epic-view.adoc[Epic View]); both are read-only: no live updates, and no action but _Chat…_, which `a` offers with `fix-relay has ended: its view, and a chat about it` (xref:19-actions.adoc#chat-ended[A Chat About Ended Work]). `a` on a row of ended work opens the same menu. `plan <plan_id>` and `epic <epic_id>` print an ended plan or epic the same way, with `ended` in its first line.
