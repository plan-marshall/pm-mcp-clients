[#actions-keys]
=== Keys of the Actions

The mnemonics of the action menu, of the acts of a hold, and of the review, one letter per link relation, in the catalogue like the keys of xref:13-texts-operator.adoc#texts-operator-keys[Keys]; `e) edit`, `b) back`, `y) yes`, and `n) no` are the common ones there, and _instruct again_ and _discard_ are those of the decomposition (xref:21-texts-starting.adoc#starting-keys[Starting Work, Keys]). No mnemonic of the menu is `a`, in any language. _Chat_ is `h` in English and `t` in German, the first free letter of its label: `c` is taken in the menu of a plan that holds (_add the credential_, in English also _accept the change_), `h` in German by _pause_. Within each menu and prompt every key is unique, in both languages.
