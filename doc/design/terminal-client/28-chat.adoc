Status: `DRAFT`. The terminal's part of the chat that the operator decided on 2026-11-09 (D5): what the role knows, the starters and the transcript's pages that D4 left open, the status lines of what has acts of its own, and what happened to the subject while the chat stays. The dialogue itself — sending, leaving, `new`, the reply line by line, `s` to stop, `Accept?` — is xref:19-actions.adoc#chat[A Chat], and it holds for every place a chat is opened (a plan, an epic, a review, the decomposition, a decision, a start, ended work). The conventions of the transcripts are those of xref:05-transcripts-entry.adoc#transcripts[Transcripts].

[#opening]
== Opening: What the Role Knows, the Starters, Older Turns

[source,text]
----
pm › plan chat fix-relay
Chat · fix-relay · with replan · the whole plan          an empty line leaves it
  replan knows: the description, 17 tasks, phase execute, version 7 · as of 10:42
  Ask replan about the whole plan.
  1) What is the riskiest open task?   2) Propose an additional test.   3) Why is it in this phase?
  you     › 1
  you     › What is the riskiest open task?▏
----

* *What the role knows* is the line under the head (`control.chat.knows`), from the chat's context summary; it comes with every turn of the role and is printed again above the reply when what it names changed (a task's status, the phase, the version), with its new time.
* *An empty chat* prints `control.chat.empty` and the scope's starters numbered (`control.chat.starter.<scope>`). A first line that is only the number of a starter puts its text into the line, where it can be changed; nothing is sent. Once the chat has a turn, a number is a message like any other.
* *A chat with turns* prints its newest page (50 turns, link:../../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#chat-page[Chat Transcript Page]); `older` as the whole first line (German `älter`, `operator.actions.chat.older_command`) prints the page before above the prompt, until `This is the beginning of the chat.` (`operator.actions.chat.no_older`). `↑` stays the line editor's history (xref:04-console.adoc#line-editor[Line Editor]), so the pages have a word of their own.
* *Reserved first lines*: a first line that is exactly one of these, compared as typed, is the command, never a message — `q` (leaves, as an empty first line does) and `new`/`neu` (xref:19-actions.adoc#chat[A Chat]), `older`/`älter`, `changes`/`änderungen`, `o`, and `o` followed by an id (`o Q-007`, <<status-lines>>). Every other line, one that only begins with such a word included, is the operator's message; to send a reserved word itself, type it with a space before it (` changes`), which is sent without that space.

[#status-lines]
== Status Lines: the Chat Shows, the View Acts

The chat carries out one act, its proposal; every other act stays in its view (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#chat-acts[Acts Stay in Their Views]). Where a reply or a notice names a decision, a hold, the plan's queued revision, or an act the role names, a status line follows it:

[source,text]
----
  replan  TASK-6 waits for your answer to Q-007; the plan cannot go on before.
          If you want it to stop meanwhile, pause it after the current task.
    ◆ Q-007 · open · blocks the plan                               o) open it
    ‖ Pause fix-relay — in the acts of fix-relay      plan pause fix-relay --after-task
  you     › o Q-007
Left the chat; it stays with fix-relay.
Question Q-007 · plan fix-relay · execute · blocks the plan · can be undone
  …
Rebase onto main? [Enter]   other: 1-3   w) in my own words   m) add a note   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   ›
----

* *The line* shows the target's mark and id and its state in the words of its view, kept current while the chat is open; it never offers the target's acts.
* *`o`* as the whole first line opens the target of the last status line printed, also one printed by `older` or `changes`, `o <id>` that of another, and with no status line printed says so (`operator.actions.chat.nothing_to_open`) (`operator.actions.key.open`, `o` in both languages). It leaves the chat, which stays as after an empty line (`Left the chat; it stays with fix-relay.`), and opens the target where its view is: a decision its answer dialogue (xref:14-transcripts-decisions.adoc[Decisions]), a hold its dialogue (xref:19-actions.adoc#holds[What Holds a Plan]), the queued revision the plan view, and an act the view of the subject that owns it with the actions of `a` — a plan's act the plan view, an epic's act (`next_plan`, `auto`, `decompose`, `archive`, `cancel_epic`) the epic's view (xref:19-actions.adoc#full-screen[In the Full-Screen View], xref:19-actions.adoc#epic-view[An Epic's View]). `h) chat` there, or `plan chat`, returns to the chat where it was.
* *An act the role names* is printed with the command that does it; the chat never runs it, and the operator runs it after leaving, with its checks and its confirmation. The reference names the act and its subject only, so the command carries no further argument and the verb asks for what it needs (xref:19-actions.adoc#commands[Commands]): `pause` `plan pause <plan_id>`, `start` `plan start <plan_id>`, `cancel` `plan cancel <plan_id>`, `abandon` `plan abandon <plan_id>`, `settings` `config <aspect> --plan <plan_id>` with the aspect asked, `next_plan` `epic next <epic_id>`, `auto` `epic auto <epic_id>`, `decompose` `epic decompose <epic_id>`, `archive` `epic archive <epic_id>`, `cancel_epic` `epic cancel <epic_id>`, a hold's act `answer` at its hold (xref:19-actions.adoc#holds[What Holds a Plan]).
* *A queued revision*: after `Enter` at `Accept?` on a running plan the chat prints `✓ accepted: applies after the current task` (as today); the plan's row and its view say `Revision from the chat queued · applies after TASK-4` until it is applied (link:../shared/plan-view.adoc#chat-revision[Plan View, A Revision from the Chat]), and the chat prints its applied line among the notices.

[#notices]
== What Happened Meanwhile

While the chat is open it follows its subject's stream (the plan's, the epic's, the entry's, the scope's of a decision, the workspace's for ended work), so a turn, a proposal or the end from another device and the subject's notices arrive as in the web app:

[source,text]
----
  you     › Is TASK-4 still the slow one?
    ▶  replan answers
  replan  Yes. It runs the transport tests twice, once per transport.
  – TASK-4 done · 10:52
  – Q-009 opened: blocks the plan · 10:53
    ◆ Q-009 · open · blocks the plan                               o) open it
  – 5 changes to fix-relay · changes shows them
  you     ›
----

* *A notice line* starts with `–`, muted, with its time (`control.chat.line.*`), printed above the prompt (xref:04-console.adoc#line-editor[Line Editor]) and never inside a reply being printed: lines that arrive while a reply is printed wait until it ends. A line that names a decision or a hold is followed by its status line.
* *More than three lines of one notice turn* (the lines the runtime writes together between two replies) fold into one line, `5 changes to fix-relay · changes shows them` (`operator.actions.chat.lines_folded`); `changes` as the whole first line (German `änderungen`, `operator.actions.chat.changes_command`) prints them.
* *Without cursor control* (`TERM=dumb`, a screen reader) the lines are printed between lines, at the next prompt, as every notice of the console.
* *A proposal overtaken*: at `Accept?`, `Enter` on a plan's proposal made against an older version prints `not applied: fix-relay changed since (version 8)` (`control.chat.changed_since`) and asks again with `r) reject` only, so the proposal can still be dropped; a proposal the plan can no longer take prints `fix-relay left the time for a revision; the proposal no longer applies.` (`control.chat.lapsed`) in place of its `Accept?`, which wins where both hold; the chat goes on.
* *A paused plan's chat* runs as every other: a reply changes nothing.
