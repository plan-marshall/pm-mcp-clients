[#texts]
== Texts

Every text of the onboarding and of the entry in the terminal is a key of the message catalogue with its English and German text (link:../shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Texts and Languages, Keys and Packs]). Arguments are ICU arguments, provisionally (link:../shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Texts and Languages, Keys and Packs]); a plural is written `{n, plural, one {…} other {…}}`. German addresses the operator with _du_, and the words of the glossary stay as they are (link:../shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#glossary[Glossary]).

* *Shared with the web app*: the keys of the areas `common` and `onboard` are the texts both clients show; the web app adds keys of its own for what only it needs (a button, a tooltip), and the terminal adds the keys of the area `operator` for what only it needs (the prompt line, the keys, the result lines). The labels of links are shared: a button in the web app and a key's label in the terminal read the same.
* *Titles are questions or names, links are actions* (link:../shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#style[How Texts Are Written]). The prompt of a primary link is the action as a question (`operator.prompt.primary` wraps the shared label: `{action}? [Enter]`).
* The texts of these flows are in the packs of the areas `common`, `onboard` (`onboarding.*.properties`), `operator`, and `error`, linked in each section below; the packs are their source. Where a text has a plural, the German has its own forms.

[#texts-common]
=== `common`: Shared by Every Flow

The texts are in the packs link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/common.en.properties[`common.en.properties`] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/common.de.properties[`common.de.properties`]; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/error.en.properties[`error.en.properties`] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/error.de.properties[`error.de.properties`]; the packs are the source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.

The `common.consent.*` keys are the texts of the consent state of both clients (link:../shared/consents.adoc#pattern[Consents, The Consent State]); the title of each consent kind, `common.consent.kind.<kind>`, is defined with the kinds (link:../shared/consents.adoc#texts[Consents, Texts]).

The `error.*` keys are the texts of the error codes the onboarding, the entry, and the console can meet. `error.already_answered` is also the line the console prints when another channel answered the state it shows, and the line of `act_not_available` for a link of a state left meanwhile, with `{channel}` one of the shared `decision.channel.web` and `decision.channel.cli`; `error.web_listener_conflict` names the key of the listener that did not open, `web.port` or `web.lan_port`; `error.credential_store_unavailable` is the text of `precondition_unmet` with `facts.reason: credential_store_unavailable`. The texts of the other error codes of `pm-api` belong to the flows that raise them and are added with them.

[#texts-onboard-auto]
==== Automatic Run

`auto.consent` lists each consent of the run as the title of its kind (`common.consent.kind.<kind>`, link:../shared/consents.adoc#texts[Consents, Texts]) with its detail (`onboard.auto.consent.detail.<kind>`), or `onboard.auto.consent.remembered` where the kind is remembered (link:../shared/consents.adoc#auto[Consents, The Automatic Run's One Consent]). Its tokens line is `onboard.auto.consent.tokens`, `{estimate}` the expected tokens of one headless login call (`common.consent.tokens.estimate`), where a harness the profiles know has no login probe; only where none lacks one is it `common.consent.tokens.none`.

The texts are in the packs link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/onboarding.en.properties[`onboarding.en.properties`] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/onboarding.de.properties[`onboarding.de.properties`]; the packs are the source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.
