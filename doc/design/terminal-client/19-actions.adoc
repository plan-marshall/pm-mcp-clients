[#commands]
== Commands

----
  plan pause <plan_id> [--now|--after-task|--after-phase]
  plan start <plan_id>
  plan cancel <plan_id> [--reason <text>] [--close-pr|--keep-pr]   on an integrity pause, its abort
  plan abandon <plan_id> [--reason <text>] [--close-pr|--keep-pr]
  plan chat <plan_id>                    the chat with the plan's planning role; on an ended plan, about its archive
  config <aspect> --plan <plan_id>       the plan's settings, in every state until it has ended (plan-defaults with
                                         posture and base branch, roles, integrations, providers)
  epic <epic_id>                         the epic's view
  epic next <epic_id> [--raise-scope|--no-raise]
  epic auto <epic_id> on|off              whether the epic starts its plans by itself
  epic auto <epic_id> --common-files <n>  how many files a plan may share with a running plan and still start
  epic decompose <epic_id> [--instruction <text>]   decompose again, with the review
  epic archive <epic_id>
  epic cancel <epic_id> [--reason <text>] [--with-plans|--without-plans]
  epic findings <epic_id>                the findings review
  epic watches <epic_id>                 the watches review
  epic chat <epic_id>                    the chat with the epic's planning role; on an ended epic, about its archive
----

* All are operator verbs, in the console and as one shell command, and direct verbs: an argument answers its state, a missing one is asked (the reason of a cancel or an abandon too), and `--no-input` stops there (xref:01-commands.adoc#direct-verbs[A Direct Verb Runs a Sub-Workflow]).
* An action the state does not allow is refused with the state and the actions it allows (`fix-relay is paused: start, abandon, chat, settings`).
* *Settings* is an action of every state of a plan until it has ended: `config <aspect> --plan <plan_id>` sets the plan's own value of an aspect, where the plan first needs it or later (xref:02-levels.adoc#level-model[Machine, Project, Plan]; <<settings>>).
* `plan cancel` on a plan held by an integrity pause is that pause's abort: its dialogue is `Abort fix-relay?`, and the plan ends `aborted` (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#confirm-end[Ending a Plan]); its epic is told `cancelled` (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#epic-told[The Epic Is Told]). A waiting or held plan is cancelled like a running one.
* `pm-operator abort plan` and `abort epic` are the shell forms of the specification: they do what `plan cancel` (or `abandon`, by the plan's state) and `epic cancel` do.
* The reviews and the chat are dialogues; as one shell command they need a terminal, like every dialogue.

[#full-screen]
== In the Full-Screen View (`plan.actions`, `epic.actions`)

`a` on a plan or an epic in the overview (xref:15-overview.adoc[The Overview]), in the plan view (xref:16-plan-view.adoc[The Plan View]), or in the epic's view (<<epic-view>>) opens its actions below the row:

----
▶ fix-relay              execute · running · 25 min · #214    relay-hardening
      p  Pause…
      x  Cancel…
      h  Chat…
      o  Settings…

▶ relay-hardening        epic · running · 3 running · 1 paused · 3 staged      ! 3
      n  Next plan: relay-token-store
      u  Automatic: on
      m  Common files allowed: 0
      d  Decompose again…
      f  Findings review (3 new)
      w  Watches review (5)
      h  Chat…
      A  Archive…
      x  Cancel…
----

* *The menu is modal*: while it is open, only its mnemonics, `↑` `↓` with `Enter`, and `a` or `Esc` (which close it) act; the keys of the view wait until it is closed.
* *Its mnemonics* are texts of the catalogue, `operator.actions.key.*` (xref:22-texts-actions.adoc#actions-keys[Keys]), in the language of the client; none is `a`, which closes the menu in every language.
* *What it offers* follows the state, from the runtime (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#plan-actions[A Plan's Actions], link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#epic-actions[An Epic's Actions]): a running plan, and one waiting for you, _Pause…_, _Cancel…_, _Chat…_, _Settings…_, the waiting one with the decision first (`!`); a pausing plan _Start_, _Cancel…_, _Chat…_, _Settings…_; a paused plan _Start_, _Abandon…_, _Chat…_, _Settings…_; a held plan those of a running one below the acts of its hold (<<holds>>), whose _end the plan_ is the menu's _Cancel…_ (on an integrity pause _Abort…_); an epic its actions by its state, _Archive…_ shown disabled while it lands (`A  Archive… (the ledger is landing)`); an ended plan or epic _Chat…_ alone (`fix-relay has ended: its view, and a chat about it`; <<chat-ended>>).
* An action with `…` leaves the view for its dialogue and returns to it afterwards; _Start_ and _Next plan_ act in place and the row follows. _Settings…_ asks which aspect (`plan-defaults`, `roles`, `integrations`, `providers`; <<settings>>) and runs `config <aspect> --plan <plan_id>`.
* A plan's own pull request is shown by its number (`#214`), a terminal hyperlink (OSC 8) where the terminal shows one; the dialogues print its URL.

[#plan]
== A Plan (`plan.confirm-end`, `plan.end`, `plan.pause-when`, `plan.pausing`, `plan.inform-epic`)

[source,text]
----
pm › x  (the menu's Cancel…)
Cancel fix-relay?
  Its epic       relay-hardening is told
  Afterwards     the plan is archived as cancelled; its branch stays
  Reason         › the approach is replaced by plan relay-v2
  Pull request
  1) close it, with the reason
  2) leave it open
  › 1
Chosen: close it, with the reason
Cancel the plan?
y) yes   n) no   q) leave   ?   › y
✓ fix-relay cancelled and archived
✓ relay-hardening told
pm ›
----

`plan cancel <plan>` on its own asks the same reason and the same consent, without the epic and
afterwards lines and without the pull request, whose answer there is `--close-pr` or `--keep-pr`.

[source,text]
----
pm › plan pause fix-relay
Where does fix-relay hold?
  1) now                       running work is withdrawn at once; it runs again on start
  2) after the current task ←  the running task finishes, then the plan holds
  3) after the current phase   the running phase finishes, then the plan holds
› ⏎
Chosen: after the current task
✓ fix-relay pausing after the task
…
✓ fix-relay paused
pm ›
----

* `plan pause` without an answer asks; `--after-task` is the answer of the default, and `--no-input` without one stops.
* `plan start` asks nothing (`plan.start`): a paused plan runs on from where it rests, a pausing one has its pause withdrawn.
* `plan cancel` and `plan abandon` ask the reason when `--reason` is missing; it is required, and `--no-input` without it stops. Afterwards the consent asks for the act the runtime performs in the plan's state (_Cancel the plan_, _Abandon the plan_, or _Abort the plan_ on an integrity pause): `y` sends, `n` and `q` send nothing, and Enter has no default.

[#settings]
=== A Plan's Settings (`plan-settings.open`, `plan-settings.aspect`, `plan-settings.value`, `plan-settings.validate`, `plan-settings.apply`)

`config <aspect> --plan <plan_id>` is the terminal's form of link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#settings[A Plan's Settings]; `config show <aspect> --plan <plan_id>` lists the plan's values with their origin, and `o) settings` in the menu asks the aspect first (`plan-defaults`, `roles`, `integrations`, `providers`). The plan defaults hold the plan's posture and base branch beside the landing, method, and asking axes; a value set here before the plan first needs it is not asked in `init.options`. `integrations` sets the plan's variant of each git and provider integration.

[source,text]
----
pm › config roles --plan fix-relay
Roles · plan fix-relay · Give a role of this plan another harness or model
  …
Applies where fix-relay next reads it; the plan's version becomes 7.
  relay-hardening is told of a change

pm › config plan-defaults --plan fix-relay
Plan defaults · plan fix-relay
  Landing   1) landing.pull-request ← from the project   2) landing.main   3) landing.files
  …   › 2
landing.main is refused: the base branch of pm-mcp-clients is protected
  Landing   1) landing.pull-request ← from the project   2) landing.main   3) landing.files
  …   › 3
Too late: fix-relay is landing; the new version would not contain where it is. Nothing changed.
pm ›
----

* A refused value prints its reason and asks the value again; `--no-input` stops there with the reason.
* *Too late* prints its reason and changes nothing; the plan goes on with what it has.
* *Changed meanwhile* (`conflict`, another client changed the value first) prints `Changed meanwhile: it is now <value>. Yours was not applied.` and asks `Apply yours again? [Enter]   k) keep the current value` (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#conflict[Changed in Two Clients at Once]).

[#epic]
== An Epic (`epic.next-work`, `epic.raise-scope`, `epic.archive-check`, `epic.archive`, `epic.confirm-cancel`, `epic.cancel`)

[source,text]
----
pm › epic next relay-hardening
✓ relay-reconnect-backoff started (2 of 3 at once)

pm › epic next relay-hardening
relay-hardening runs 3 of 3 at once; the next plan, relay-token-store, waits for a free place.
  r) Raise to 4 at once and start it   n) leave it   ?   › r
✓ relay-token-store started (4 of 4 at once)

pm › epic archive relay-hardening
Archive relay-hardening?
    ✓  no plan running, pausing, or paused
    ✓  every plan shipped or ended            11 shipped · 1 cancelled
    ✗  open findings, not decided yet         2   (epic findings relay-hardening)
    ✓  the ledger is landed
    ✓  the completion review is done
    ·  carried into the archive: 3 kept findings · 5 watches
relay-hardening cannot be archived yet: decide what the check names first.

pm › epic archive relay-docs
Archive relay-docs?
    ·  the completion review runs first
    ✓  no plan running, pausing, or paused
    ✓  every plan shipped or ended            4 shipped
    ✓  open findings, not decided yet         0
    ✓  the ledger is landed
    ✓  the completion review is done
    ·  carried into the archive: 1 kept finding · 2 watches
Archive the epic? y) yes   n) not yet   ?   › y
✓ relay-docs archived
pm ›
----

* With no plan launchable, `epic next` says why per row, by one of the reasons `dependency`, `lock`, `scope`, `overlap`, `stale`, and `held` (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#epic-actions[An Epic's Actions]; `relay-auth-v2 waits for relay-reconnect-backoff`, `relay-docs waits for a lock: the surface pm-relay`); with only the scope full, it asks to raise the scope by one (`epic.raise-scope`), which `--raise-scope` answers yes and `--no-raise` no; `--no-input` without either stops.
* With none launchable for an overlap, the reason names the running plan (`relay-docs waits: overlap with relay-auth-v2`); raising the scope does not start it.
* `epic cancel` asks first (`epic.confirm-cancel`, link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#cancel-epic[Cancelling an Epic]): its plans cancelled too or left to run on (`--with-plans`, `--without-plans`), and the reason; confirmed, the epic is archived as cancelled (`epic.cancel`).
* `epic archive` while the epic lands is refused with the reason (`relay-hardening is landing: archive waits until the ledger has landed`); only open findings hold the archive, and kept findings and watches are listed as carried.
* On an epic whose completion review has not run yet (a manual one; an automatic epic has run it once it was through), `epic archive` runs the review first (`epic.review-completion`, link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#archive-check[Archiving]), prints `the completion review runs first` (`control.archive.review_first`), and prints the check below it once the review is done; with every check passing it asks _Archive the epic_ (`control.archive.action`, `n` is `control.archive.not_yet`) and prints `operator.actions.result.archived`. `q` and `Ctrl+C` leave while it runs, the review going on (xref:03-dialogues.adoc#resume[Interruption and Resume]); `epic archive` again shows the check once it is done. A review that stages further plans leaves the epic not through, and the check names them.

[#epic-auto]
=== Automatic On or Off (`epic.auto`)

`epic auto <epic_id> on|off` and `u` in the menu below an epic's row switch whether the epic starts its plans by itself (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#epic-auto[Automatic: On or Off]); no confirmation, since the other value undoes it. The verb names the value, never a flip, and `u` in the menu sends the value it does not show.

[source,text]
----
pm › epic auto relay-hardening on
✓ relay-hardening starts its plans by itself; relay-token-store started (2 of 3 at once)

pm › epic auto relay-hardening on
relay-hardening already starts its plans by itself.

pm › epic auto relay-hardening off
✓ relay-hardening starts no plan by itself any more; what runs goes on.
----

* *Common files* (`epic auto <epic_id> --common-files <n>`, `m` in the menu below an epic's row, beside the toggle): how many files a plan may share with a running plan and still start beside it, the epic's `overlap_max_files`, taken from the project's `epic.overlap_max_files` when the epic started. One act naming the value it sets, told among the epic's events, and the epic at once starts what the new value admits (`✓ relay-hardening allows 2 common files; relay-docs started`, `control.epic.overlap_done`); the head of `epic <epic_id>` shows it (`Common files allowed: 0`), and the project's default is changed in the project's settings (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#epic-auto[Automatic: On or Off]).
* Switched on with nothing launchable, the line says why (`Nothing can start now: relay-docs waits: overlap with relay-auth-v2`, `control.epic.auto_nothing`).
* The head of `epic <epic_id>` names the mode (`automatic`, `manual`), and its events what the epic did by itself after a plan ended (link:../shared/epic-view.adoc#reactions[Epic View, What the Epic Does by Itself]).

[#epic-view]
== An Epic's View

`epic <epic_id>` prints the epic's view as a tree into the scrollback (link:../shared/epic-view.adoc[Epic View], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#UC-PLN-5[UC-PLN-5]); in the full-screen view, `Enter` on an epic opens it as its level, with the workstreams, the sources, the landings, and the events opening like nodes of the plan view, its header the path `pm-mcp-clients › relay-hardening`, `a` for its actions, and `p` for its project (key line `operator.plan.keys.project`), the project's level of the overview, one step away (link:../shared/epic-view.adoc#layout[Epic View]); `Enter` on a plan opens its plan view, whose `e` comes back to the epic (xref:16-plan-view.adoc#full-screen-keys[The Plan View, Keys]), and `Esc` goes back to where the epic's view was opened.

* *The head*: the title, the state (`running`, `waiting for you`, `held`, `landing`, `ended`), how many plans run of how many may, since when it runs, and the count of what needs the operator; below it the epic's project with the command that opens it (`operator.plan.tree.context`, xref:16-plan-view.adoc#plan-view-texts[The Plan View, Texts]); what waits stands first below that, each with the command that opens it.
* *The workstreams*: each plan with its status as link:../shared/epic-view.adoc#layout[Epic View] lists it: the queue row's status (`staged`, `running`, `pausing`, `paused`, `handed back`, `shipped` for a row `merged` or `completed`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, and the row's other ended statuses by their names), with `waiting for you` and `held` shown from its plan; a plan aborted from an integrity pause is `abandoned` here and keeps its outcome `aborted`, and a plan released with its project changes no row (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#epic-told[The Epic Is Told]), what it waits for (a dependency, a lock, the scope, an overlap with a running plan), and since when. * *Sources, landings, findings, watches, events*: the sources as stored; each landing with its pull request and the epic's analysis; the open items with their counts and the review that opens them; the events, newest first, the ten newest in the tree and all of them in the full-screen view.
* *Tokens*: the sum of the epic's plans and of its own tasks (decompose, landing, review), with the split, as the runtime sums it (link:../shared/epic-view.adoc#layout[Epic View]); a floor with `≥` and the count of tasks not measured where there are any; never money, and no budget of its own. `epic <epic_id> --tokens` prints the figure per plan and the epic's own after the tree, each plan with the command that opens it; in the full-screen view the line opens like a node.
* An ended epic prints as it ended, read-only, with `ended` and its end (`completed`, `cancelled`, `released`) in its first line (xref:15-overview.adoc#ended[Ended Work]).

[#holds]
== What Holds a Plan (`hold.show`, `hold.resolve`, `hold.apply`)

[source,text]
----
pm › plan fix-relay
fix-relay · execute · held · holds: a condition since 14:02
  cause   the plan reads cui-http, which this project may not read
  tried   3 times, last 14:10
  g) grant read access to cui-http…   r) retry   x) end the plan…   ?   › g
----

[source,text]
----
pm › plan relay-cursor-order
relay-cursor-order · execute · held · holds: an integrity pause since 10:31
  cause   the plan's store file references.json changed outside the runtime
  recorded  4,812 bytes   sha256 3f9a…c21e
  current   5,034 bytes   sha256 b70d…9a44
  c) accept the change…   x) abort the plan…   ?   › c
Accept references.json as it is?   its digest is recorded anew and the plan goes on with it; this cannot be undone
  y) yes   n) no   ?   › y
✓ relay-cursor-order goes on with the change
----

The acts of a hold are those of the shared design (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#holds[What Holds a Plan]), each with its key; the terminal offers them on `plan` and `epic`, and in the menu below a row with `a`:

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Hold |Its prompt

|stalled
|`r) retry now   w) recycle the worker   x) end the plan…`

|condition
|the act it names as a dialogue: `g) grant read access to <project>…`, `c) add the credential <name>…`, or `b) raise the budget…`; then `r) retry   x) end the plan…`

|integrity pause
|the tampered file with its recorded and current size and digest; `c) accept the change…   x) abort the plan…` (an epic's: `x) cancel the epic…`); no diff and no restore, since the prior content is not kept

|held item of an epic
|`r) retry` and the item's own acts, `x) end the plan…` for a plan's item, else `x) cancel the epic…`

|landing failed (an epic's)
|`r) retry   p) show the pull request   x) cancel the epic…`

|landing failed (a move's removal from git)
|`r) retry   p) show the pull request   g) give up the removal…`
|===

* *After the act* (`hold.apply`): released (`hold.released`), the line `✓ <subject> goes on` (`control.hold.released`), `<subject>` the plan, the epic, or the landing that was held; still held, `hold.show` prints the hold again with `Still held: <cause>` (`control.hold.still_held`) and its prompt.
* The labels are the shared keys `control.hold.act.<act>`, the kind of the hold the shared `control.hold.kind.<kind>`; the mnemonics are `operator.actions.key.<act>` (xref:22-texts-actions.adoc#actions-keys[Keys]).
* The holds are exactly these five; a paused plan is none and offers its actions only.
* An act that cannot be undone (_accept the change_, _end the plan_, _abort the plan_, _cancel the epic_, _give up the removal_) confirms first and names what follows; _abort the plan_ is the dialogue `Abort fix-relay?` of `plan cancel`. _Give up the removal_ keeps the moved files tracked in git: the removal's branch is dropped, and the source keeps its copy.
* _Show the pull request_ prints its number and URL. The path of a tampered file is printed after the control characters are removed (xref:04-console.adoc#output-safety[Output Safety]).

* `k) keep` asks whether as a finding or to the watches (in the watches review: as a watch or to the findings), the item's register preselected.
* `e) edit` at `Stage it?` asks what to change in the drafted plan (`review.instruct-plan`); the planning role drafts it again (`review.redraft-plan`) and the read-back shows old against new (`review.read-back-plan`): `Enter` takes the new draft, `i)` instructs again, `x)` keeps the draft before; each returns to `Stage it?`.
* `e) edit` on an item works the same on its statement and rules (`review.instruct`, `review.rewrite`, `review.read-back`): `Enter` applies the change, `x)` leaves the item unchanged. The operator's words are not interpreted: the planning role works from them, and only its proposal is read back.
* `d) drop` asks the reason in one line; it is recorded.
* `r) accept the remaining` lists every item not decided yet with its proposal and confirms once.
* *An item from outside the machine* (a finding from a review comment) carries `untrusted`; its text is printed as text between quotes, never rendered, after the control characters are removed.
* `h) chat` asks its scope, `1) this item ←   2) the whole register`, and opens the chat (<<chat>>); an accepted change becomes the item's disposition or its rewrite.
* `f) finish` writes the decisions to the epic's ledger. Leaving the review before (`q`, `Ctrl+D`) keeps what was decided; `epic findings relay-hardening` opens it where it stopped.

[#chat]
== A Chat (`chat.open`, `chat.turn`, `chat.read-back`, `chat.apply`, `chat.end`)

`plan chat <plan_id>` and `epic chat <epic_id>` open the chat of link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#chat[Chatting with the Planning Role]; `h) chat` in the menu below a row, in the review (<<review>>), in the decomposition (xref:18-starting.adoc#decomposition[The Decomposition]), at a decision's prompt (xref:14-transcripts-decisions.adoc#t-decision-chat[A Chat Before Answering]), at the list of sources (xref:18-starting.adoc#chat[Chatting About the Request]), and on ended work (<<chat-ended>>) opens the same dialogue with its scope (link:../shared/work-actions.adoc#chat-places[The Same Chat in Three More Places]).

[source,text]
----
pm › plan chat fix-relay
Chat · fix-relay · with replan · the whole plan            an empty line ends it
  you     › Can the cursor test also cover the SSE transport?
    ▶  replan answers
  replan  Yes. TASK-4 tests one transport only; I propose a second test.
  Proposed change   applies after the current task
  - TASK-4  cursor write order: a test for the websocket transport
  + TASK-4  cursor write order: a test per transport, websocket and SSE
  Its epic   relay-hardening is told
Accept? [Enter]   r) reject   ?   › ⏎
    ✓  accepted: applies after the current task; relay-hardening told
  you     › ⏎
✓ The chat is kept with fix-relay.
…
✓ fix-relay changed; the plan's version becomes 8
pm ›
----

* *A message* is one line or several, sent by an empty line; an empty line as its first line ends the chat, as `q` and `Ctrl+D` do. A proposal not yet accepted is dropped with it. A message is never interpreted or read back: the role answers it, and only a change it proposes is read back.
* *The answer* is printed as text after the control characters are removed (xref:04-console.adoc#output-safety[Output Safety]); an item from outside the machine stays marked `untrusted` and is printed between quotes.
* *A proposed change* is a read-back, old against new: `Enter` accepts, `r) reject` drops it; a check that fails prints its reason (`not applied: …`) and the chat goes on.
* *A running plan* takes an accepted revision at its next task boundary, and the line after it says so once it is applied; a pausing plan takes it at that boundary too and then holds at its point; a paused plan takes it when the operator starts it again (`applies when fix-relay starts again`). A question changes nothing.
* *When a revision is proposed*: only in Phase 5 (`execute`) or in finalize before the merge; earlier, and after the merge, the role answers and proposes none, and the chat says so once (`fix-relay proposes no change yet: a revision comes in execute or in finalize, until its merge`).
* *Chat is refused* with its reason while no configuration of the routed role qualifies (`no configuration of replan qualifies yet`), and `--no-input` refuses it, since every turn needs the operator.

----

          provider's tests waited for the relay's new event API.
  you     › Draft the follow-up on top of relay-v2.
    ▶  replan answers
  Proposed follow-up plan
  + Add the Codeberg issue provider on top of relay-v2.
  + follows codeberg-issues, cancelled: replaced by relay-v2
Start a plan with it? [Enter]   r) reject   ?   › ⏎
✓ The chat is kept with codeberg-issues.
A new plan is being started in pm-mcp-clients with this description; go on with its sources.

    ✓  description   "Add the Codeberg issue provider on top of relay-v2. …"
  d) description   f) documents   i) issue   l) lesson (2)   h) chat   Enter) continue   x) remove one   ?   ›
