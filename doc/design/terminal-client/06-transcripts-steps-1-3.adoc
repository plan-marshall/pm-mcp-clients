[#t-language]
== 1 Language (`language.detect`, `language.choose`, `language.record`, `language.done`)

The system's locale is `de_DE` and a German pack exists, so the state opens in German (xref:03-dialogues.adoc#language[Language of the Terminal]):

[source,text]
----
[1/7] Sprache · Wähle deine Sprachen
  plan-marshall und pm-operator      1) English   2) Deutsch ← von deinem System (de_DE)
  Was Modelle schreiben und lesen    3) wie oben ←   4) English   5) Deutsch

  Wörter wie Pull Request, Commit, Merge und Request bleiben, wie sie sind.

Verwende Deutsch für beides? [Enter]   anders: 1-5   ?   › ?
  Modelle arbeiten nicht in jeder Sprache gleich gut; du kannst sie auf
  Englisch arbeiten lassen, während plan-marshall Deutsch spricht. „wie oben"
  folgt einer späteren Änderung der ersten Sprache.
Verwende Deutsch für beides? [Enter]   anders: 1-5   ?   › 4
  Was Modelle schreiben und lesen    3) wie oben   4) English ✎   5) Deutsch
Verwende Deutsch hier, English für Modelle? [Enter]   anders: 1-5   ?   › 1
[1/7] Language · Choose your languages
  plan-marshall and pm-operator    1) English ✎   2) Deutsch   from your system (de_DE)
  What models write and read       3) same as above   4) English ✎   5) Deutsch

  Words like Pull Request, Commit, Merge and Request stay as they are.

Use English for both? [Enter]   other: 1-5   ?   › 2
[1/7] Sprache · Wähle deine Sprachen
  plan-marshall und pm-operator      1) English   2) Deutsch ← von deinem System (de_DE)
  Was Modelle schreiben und lesen    3) wie oben   4) English ✎   5) Deutsch
  …
Verwende Deutsch hier, English für Modelle? [Enter]   anders: 1-5   ?   › ⏎
✓ Sprachen gespeichert: Deutsch, Modelle English
----

* `language.detect` and `language.record` print no line of their own beyond the result; `language.done` is the result line.
* The state opens in the system's language where a pack exists (`LC_ALL`, `LC_MESSAGES`, `LANG`), else in English. A change of the interface language reprints the whole state in it before it is recorded, so the operator reads it first; a change of the model language marks the line `✎` and changes nothing else (xref:03-dialogues.adoc#language[Language of the Terminal]).

* The two consents of `catalogue.consent` are asked together and answered together or one by one (`1`, `2`); a model probe without the advice, or the advice without the probe, is a valid answer, and `catalogue.select` then shows what it has (no recommendation, or model ids to enter).
* `?0` is the recommendation's rationale, `?n` the rationale of item `n` with cost and limits.
* A harness that cannot list its models shows `a) add a model id` as its only entry; `a` asks `Model id for OpenCode › `.
* `catalogue.enrol` runs the check and the confirmation of `harness.probe` and `harness.confirm` for each further harness, under the step line of step 3.
