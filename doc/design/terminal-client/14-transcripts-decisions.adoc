[#t-decisions]
== Decisions

[#t-decision-clear]
=== Reversible, a Clear Majority (`decision.answer`, `decision.record`)

[source,text]
----
pm [1 question] › answer Q-007
Question Q-007 · plan fix-relay · execute · blocks the plan · can be undone
  The change conflicts with main in RelayServer.java.
  ? shows the evidence: the conflict, both versions of the method

    2) Rebase onto main ←          2 of 3 votes · 2 models   clear majority
    1) Merge main into the branch  1 of 3 votes · 1 model
    3) Stop the plan and ask me later
  Recommended by implement: Rebase onto main. Keeps the history linear.
  Against it (quorum-b): the branch was pushed; a rebase rewrites what others pulled.

Rebase onto main? [Enter]   other: 1-3   w) in my own words   m) add a note   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › ⏎
✓ Q-007 answered: Rebase onto main. The plan goes on.
pm ›
----

* The options keep the workflow's order; the recommended one is marked, not moved.
* `?2` shows the votes for option 2, each with its role, model, reason, and the model's own estimate, labelled as such.

[#t-decision-irreversible]
=== Not Reversible (`decision.answer`, `decision.confirm`)

[source,text]
----
pm [1 question] › answer Q-009
Question Q-009 · plan drop-legacy-api · finalize · blocks the plan · cannot be undone
  Delete the published v1 endpoints from the release branch?

    1) Delete them now                 1 of 3 votes · 1 model
    2) Deprecate them, delete in 0.11  1 of 3 votes · 1 model
    3) Keep them                       1 of 3 votes · 1 model
  Votes: split. The recommendation is one step away, so that you weigh the
  options first: ?r

Choose: 1-3   w) in my own words   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › ?r
  Recommended by architecture: Deprecate them, delete in 0.11.
  Against it (quorum-a): two clients of v1 are known; both migrated last week.
Choose: 1-3   w) in my own words   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › 2

Your decision · Q-009 · cannot be undone
  Deprecate the v1 endpoints, delete them in 0.11.
  Follows: a deprecation header on every v1 response; a task for 0.11.
Decide? [Enter]   b) back   ?   › ⏎
✓ Q-009 answered: Deprecate them, delete in 0.11.
----

* No option is preselected and `Enter` alone asks for a choice; `decision.confirm` restates what follows before the answer is sent.

[part 2 of 3] How is it migrated?          depends on part 1: A file per plan

    2) By a verb the operator runs
At the first start? [Enter]   other: 1-2   b) back   ?   › 2

    1) JSON lines ←
    2) SQLite per plan
JSON lines? [Enter]   other: 1-2   b) back   ?   › b
[part 2 of 3] How is it migrated?          depends on part 1: A file per plan
    2) By a verb the operator runs ✎

[#t-decision-own-words]
=== In Own Words (`decision.interpret`, `decision.read-back`, `decision.clarify`)

[source,text]
----
Rebase onto main? [Enter]   other: 1-3   w) in my own words   m) add a note   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › w
Your answer (Enter sends it): › rebase, but leave release/0.9 alone, it is frozen
  ⠴  interpret is reading your answer       0:03

I understood: Rebase onto main, provided release/0.9 stays as it is.
  your words: rebase, but leave release/0.9 alone, it is frozen
              ^^^^^^          ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Correct? [Enter]   e) edit my words   o) choose an option instead   ?   › ⏎
✓ Q-007 answered: Rebase onto main, provided release/0.9 stays as it is.
----

Unclear, then a new option in the operator's words:

[source,text]
----
Your answer (Enter sends it): › do whatever the reviewer would do
  ⠴  interpret is reading your answer       0:02
interpret asks: Which reviewer do you mean, and what would they do here: merge
  main into the branch, or something else?
Your reply (Enter sends it): › neither: squash the branch into one commit on main
  ⠴  interpret is reading your answer       0:03

I understood: your own answer: squash the branch into one commit on main.
Correct? [Enter]   e) edit my words   o) choose an option instead   ?   › ⏎
✓ Q-007 answered in your own words.
----

* The read-back marks the passages it rests on; nothing is recorded before `Enter`.
* A delegation is read back as `you leave it to the recommendation: …` and offered only where the part allows one; the part's recommendation is then taken as the answer, recorded as `delegated`.
* `decision.clarify` shows the question and takes the reply alone, which is added to the words and interpreted again. It asks at most as often as the decision's unit declares (by default twice). A read-back of an understood answer always offers `e)` and `o)`; once the bound is reached, or the interpreting task ends without a result, the decision returns to `decision.answer` with the part unanswered and the reason (`decision.interpret.unclear_bound`), and the console offers the options and `w)` again.

[#t-decision-ends]
=== Later, None Fits, Changed

[source,text]
----
…   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › l
Q-007 stays open; the plan waits for it. questions lists it.
pm [1 question] › answer Q-007
…   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › x
None fits: the plan thinks again and asks anew. Send? [Enter]   b) back   › ⏎
✓ Q-007: you asked the plan to think again.
pm ›
◆ Question Q-009 · plan fix-relay · blocks the plan · asked anew after Q-007
  …
  answer Q-009   ·   questions
pm [1 question] ›
◆ Q-009 changed: a third option was added. Please answer again.
pm [1 question] ›
----

* _Later_ (`l`) leaves the decision open and announced; the console leaves it, and the badge keeps counting it.
* _None fits_ (`x`) is offered only where the decision names a role that asked it, which can ask anew, or where a quorum is declared that can vote again; a decision a server rule raised, a quorum escalated, or the setup's workflow asked, with no quorum declared, shows no `x)` and stays open until it is answered (link:../shared/decisions.adoc[Decisions]). Where offered, it closes the decision as withdrawn, with the note where `m)` added one before; the role that asked thinks again and asks anew as a new decision, announced like any other with the one it replaces (`decision.outcome.asked_anew`).
* No decision expires: the plan waits until the operator answers it, and `questions` shows since when.
* *Refused answers*, each printed with the decision as it stands and never as an error of the console: `conflict` (an answer to an older revision: the changed decision is printed with what changed, as above, and asks again); `already_answered` (the other client was first: the line of xref:04-console.adoc#answered-elsewhere[Answered Elsewhere] says where and how); `invalid_answer` (the answer the runtime refuses, with its reason at the part) and `dependency_unanswered` (a part whose dependency is still open, named at the part) keep the decision open at its prompt (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#api-error-codes[Local API Error Codes]).

[#t-decision-chat]
=== A Chat Before Answering (`decision.answer`, `chat.*`, `decision.interpret`)

`h) chat` opens the chat of xref:19-actions.adoc#chat[A Chat] with the decision as its scope (link:../shared/decisions.adoc#chat[Decisions, A Chat Before Answering]); its key is the chat's everywhere, `operator.actions.key.chat` (`h`, German `t`).

[source,text]
----
pm [1 question] › answer Q-007
Question Q-007 · plan fix-relay · execute · blocks the plan · can be undone
  …
Rebase onto main? [Enter]   other: 1-3   w) in my own words   m) add a note   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › h
Chat · fix-relay · with implement · decision Q-007        an empty line ends it
  you        › What does a rebase mean for the open pull request #214?
    ▶  implement answers
  implement  #214 has 2 review comments on RelayServer.java. A rebase rewrites
             the branch; the comments stay, their lines may move. That is the
             dissent of quorum-b.
  you        › Then merge, but only while release/0.9 stays untouched.
    ▶  implement answers
  Proposed answer
    merge main into the branch, as long as release/0.9 stays untouched
Answer in these words? [Enter]   r) reject   ?   › ⏎
  ⠴  interpret is reading your answer       0:02

I understood: Merge main into the branch, provided release/0.9 stays as it is.
  your words: merge main into the branch, as long as release/0.9 stays untouched
              ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^           ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Correct? [Enter]   e) edit my words   o) choose an option instead   ?   › ⏎
✓ Q-007 answered after a chat: Merge main into the branch, provided release/0.9 stays as it is.
pm ›
----

A revision that removes the need for the decision, and a chat ended without an answer:

[source,text]
----
pm [2 questions] › answer Q-016
Question Q-016 · plan storage-v2 · execute · blocks the plan · can be undone
  How is the old store migrated?
…   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › h
Chat · storage-v2 · with replan · decision Q-016          an empty line ends it
  you        › Could the plan avoid the migration altogether?
  replan     Yes: the new store can read the old files as they are.
  Proposed change
  - TASK-5  migrate the store at the first start of the new release
  + TASK-5  read the old files in place; no migration
Accept? [Enter]   r) reject   ?   › ⏎
    ✓  accepted: applied
– Q-016 is closed: the revision removed the need for it (nothing is migrated).
✓ The chat is kept with Q-016.
pm [1 question] › answer Q-014
…   h) chat   x) none fits   l) later   ?   › h
Chat · fix-relay · with replan · decision Q-014           an empty line ends it
  you        › Why is this asked now and not after the review?
  replan     The task graph needs it before TASK-7 can be planned.
  you        › ⏎
✓ The chat is kept with Q-014.
Question Q-014 · plan fix-relay · plan · blocks the plan · can be undone
  …
----

* `h) chat` stands with _none fits_ and _later_, for the whole decision; it is no answer, and the plan waits while it runs.
* The role follows who asked: Q-007 was asked by the task `implement`, so its chat goes to `implement`; Q-016, escalated by a quorum, and Q-014, raised by a server rule, go to the plan's planning role `replan`.
* A proposed answer is offered only where the part takes own words; `Enter` sends it to `interpret` as the operator's words, and the read-back follows as in <<t-decision-own-words>>. `r) reject` drops it and the chat goes on.
* A revision accepted in the chat leaves the decision open (`Q-014 stays open; answer it once the chat has ended.`) unless the runtime closes it as withdrawn, as above.
* Answered in another channel during the chat, the console prints `– Q-007 was answered in the web app (iPad); the chat has ended.` and returns to its prompt.
* While no configuration of the routed role qualifies, `h` is refused with the reason (`no configuration of implement qualifies yet`); a waiver request has no `h`.

[#t-decision-setup]
=== A Question of a Project's Setup

[source,text]
----
◆ Question Q-031 · setup of pm-mcp-clients · descriptions · blocks the setup · can be undone
pm [1 question] › answer Q-031
  Should the generated sources of pm-api be described as a module of their own?
  1) yes   2) no, with pm-api ← recommended by enrich
Answer? [Enter]   other: 1-2   w) in my own words   h) chat   l) later   ?   › l
Q-031 stays open; the setup of pm-mcp-clients waits for it. questions lists it.
----

* A decision of a project's setup is announced, listed by `questions`, and answered like a plan's; its subject is `setup of <project>` and its step stands where a plan's phase would (link:../shared/decisions.adoc#setup[Decisions, A Project's Setup Asks]). A decision of a start's entry is the same, its subject `start in <project>`.
* `h) chat` goes to the role of the task that asked (`enrich` here); for a question the setup's workflow raised itself, `h` is refused with `decision.chat.no_role`. A decision of the setup offers no _none fits_ unless it declares a quorum.

[#t-decision-waiver]
=== A Waiver Request

[source,text]
----
pm [1 waiver request] › questions
  WR-2  plan fix-relay  waiver request: ci_check:integration-tests
        "The test needs a second machine the CI does not have."   waiting since 13:10
pm [1 waiver request] › answer WR-2
Waiver request WR-2 · plan fix-relay · gate ci_check:integration-tests
  The test needs a second machine the CI does not have.
  ? shows the evidence: the failing run, the test

Waive the gate or decline the request?   y) waive, with a reason   d) decline, with a reason   l) later   ?   › ⏎
Type y, d or l.
Waive the gate or decline the request?   y) waive, with a reason   d) decline, with a reason   l) later   ?   › y
Reason (required): › covered by the manual run on the build box, log attached to the PR
✓ Waived ci_check:integration-tests for fix-relay.
----

Declined, and left for later:

[source,text]
----
pm [1 waiver request] › answer WR-3
Waiver request WR-3 · plan session-log · gate coverage:pm-api
  …
Waive the gate or decline the request?   y) waive, with a reason   d) decline, with a reason   l) later   ?   › d
Reason (required): › the missing tests are part of this plan; write them
✓ Declined WR-3: the plan takes its next step without the waiver.
pm › answer WR-4
…
Waive the gate or decline the request?   y) waive, with a reason   d) decline, with a reason   l) later   ?   › l
WR-4 stays open; the plan waits for it. questions lists it.
pm [1 waiver request] ›
----

* A waiver request is answered by a waiver or a decline, each an operator override with a required reason: no preselection, no `Enter` default, no own words, no interpretation. _Later_ leaves it open; the plan waits, as for a decision.
* An empty reason is not sent: the console asks again with `A reason is required.`

[#t-decision-texts]
=== The Texts of These Transcripts

The texts are the shared ones of link:../shared/decisions-texts.adoc#texts[Decisions, Texts] (`decision.*`, among them `decision.interpret.working` with the role `interpret`, `decision.confirm.action` for `Decide?`, `decision.outcome.rethink`, `decision.outcome.revised`, and `decision.waiver.title`, `decision.waiver.waive`, `decision.waiver.decline`, `decision.waiver.later`, `decision.waiver.reason` for the waiver request), and the console's own of <<texts-operator-decisions>> (`operator.decision.*`) for the prompts only the terminal has: `Your answer (Enter sends it):`, `Your reply (Enter sends it):`, `Your note:`, `Choose: 1-3`, `1-3) change a part`, the line of _later_, the confirmation of _none fits_, the result lines, the prompt of a proposed answer in a chat, and the prompt and results of a waiver request; the chat itself has the texts of xref:22-texts-actions.adoc#actions-texts[Actions] and `decision.chat.*`.
