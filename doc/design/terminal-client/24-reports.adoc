What a report is, what it shows, and how it is read is link:../shared/reports.adoc[Reports], for both clients; a plan's completion report is link:../shared/completion-report.adoc[Completion Report]. This part is how the console offers them. The conventions of the transcripts are those of xref:05-transcripts-entry.adoc#transcripts[Transcripts].

[#commands]
== Commands

----
  reports [--project <project_id>|.] [--older]   the unread reports, grouped, newest first; the next page
  reports read <report_id>... | --all       mark one or several read, or all those the last listing showed
  reports open <report_id>                  its detail; reads it
  plan report <plan_id> [--tokens [--components]]   an ended plan's completion report; with its tokens by phase, role, and model
----

* All are operator verbs, in the console and as one shell command; they ask nothing, so `--no-input` changes nothing, with one exception: `reports open` on a report whose detail is a start still open continues `plan add`, a direct verb.
* *A report's id* is the one the runtime gives it (`rep-0142`); `Tab` completes the ids of the unread ones.
* `reports` without a project prints every project's; `--project .` the project of the working directory.
* `reports read --all` marks read the reports the last `reports` of this console printed, by their ids (link:../shared/reports.adoc#read[Reports, Reading a Report]); as one shell command, or before any listing, it reads the list first and marks read what it read, and prints how many. A report that arrived since stays unread.
* `reports open` runs the command of the report's detail and prints it: `plan report <plan_id>` for a plan that ended, `plan <plan_id>` for a plan, `epic <epic_id>` for an epic (its landing, its rows, or its completion review first), `projects` at the project, `status --ended --project` after a release, `cleanup` for a cleanup that is due (xref:26-cleanup.adoc[Cleanup]), `plan <plan_id> --activity <task_id>` for a task the server restarted or yielded off track (`task.off_track`; xref:16-plan-view.adoc#activity[The Plan View, A Worker at Work]), the subject's view at the decision for a decision closed elsewhere (`decision.closed_elsewhere`), `plan add` continued at its sources for a flagged source of a start still open (xref:18-starting.adoc#plan[Starting a Plan]), also of the start of a follow-up plan that a chat about ended work opened (xref:19-actions.adoc#chat-ended[A Chat About Ended Work]). It reads the report in the same act. Continuing `plan add` is a direct verb (xref:01-commands.adoc#direct-verbs[A Direct Verb Runs a Sub-Workflow]): with `--no-input` it stops with exit code `3` at the first state that needs the operator and has no answer, prints that state and how to answer it, and leaves the entry open with its sources; the report is read all the same.
* Marking read a report that is already read, here or in another client, is no error: `rep-0142 was already read.` (`operator.reports.already_read`); an id that is no report of this machine is refused (`operator.reports.not_found`).
* `plan report` on a plan that has not ended is refused with its state (`fix-relay is running; its completion report follows when it ends`).

[source,text]
----
pm › reports
Reports · 5 unread
pm-mcp-clients
  relay-hardening
    rep-0142  fix-relay           Ended: merged (#214)                  today 11:20
              3 of 3 deliverables · 2 h 40 min                reports open rep-0142
    rep-0143  relay-token-store   Started by relay-hardening (2 of 3)   today 11:21
    rep-0144  relay-docs          Waits: overlap with relay-auth-v2     today 11:21
  rep-0139    docs-typo           Ended: no change                      today 09:02 · sent 09:40
pm-mcp-core
  rep-0131    project             Onboarded                             yesterday 18:40
reports read rep-0142 · reports read --all
pm › reports read --all
✓ 5 reports marked read.
pm ›
----

* *Grouped and ordered* as link:../shared/reports.adoc#list[Reports, The List] says: project, then epic, then plan; the newest first. The first line names the plan, the epic, or `project`; the second the body, with the command of its detail where it has one.
* *Sent* stands beside _created_ only where the two differ by more than a minute (`sent 09:40`).
* More than one screen of unread reports prints the newest page and the line `12 older: reports --older` (`operator.reports.older`); `reports --older` prints the next page.
* No unread report: `Nothing new. Reports you have read are not shown any more.` (`report.list.empty`).

[#arrival]
== On Start

When the console opens and there are unread reports, it prints one line after the entry's lines and before the prompt:

[source,text]
----
✓ Web app ready: http://127.0.0.1:47613
▪ 5 unread reports since yesterday 18:40 · reports shows them
pm ›
----

* The line is the console's form of link:../shared/reports.adoc#arrival[Reports, On Entering a Client]: the count and the oldest time from the runtime (`report.arrival.line`), and the command that shows them (`operator.reports.arrival_hint`).
* No unread report, no line. The prompt carries no badge for reports: its badges are what waits for the operator (xref:04-console.adoc#prompt[The Prompt]).
* After a reconnect the line `✓ Reconnected …` names the unread reports too where there are any (`operator.reports.reconnected`).

[#notice]
== While the Console Is Open

A report that arrives is the quiet line of xref:04-console.adoc#pushed[What the Runtime Pushes], printed above the prompt like a notice, without the bell and without a desktop notification:

[source,text]
----
pm › config roles --pro
▪ fix-relay: Ended: merged (#214) · reports open rep-0142
pm › config roles --pro
----

* Several arriving together are one line (`▪ 3 new reports · relay-hardening · reports`, `report.notice.many`); during a dialogue the line waits for the next state of the dialogue, as every notice does, and in the full-screen view it is summed up when the view is left (xref:15-overview.adoc#full-screen-live[Live, and Leaving]).
* A report read in another client prints nothing; a later `reports` no longer lists it.

[#full-screen]
== In the Full-Screen View

`R` in the overview (xref:15-overview.adoc#full-screen-keys[The Overview, Keys]) opens the unread reports as a level of the same view; the footer of the overview's level of projects carries the count while there are unread reports (`R 5 reports`, `operator.reports.key_count`).

----
 Reports · 5 unread                                               ● connected
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  pm-mcp-clients
    ◇ relay-hardening
▶     ▸ fix-relay          Ended: merged (#214)            today 11:20
      ▸ relay-token-store  Started by relay-hardening (2 of 3)   11:21
    ▸ docs-typo            Ended: no change                today 09:02 · sent 09:40
  pm-mcp-core
    ▪ project              Onboarded                       yesterday 18:40
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ↑↓ move   Enter details   m read   M read all shown   p project   e epic   Esc back
----

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Key |Action

|`Enter`
|the report's detail in the same view: the completion report, the plan view, the epic's view, the project; reads it. `Esc` from there comes back to the reports.

|`m`
|mark the selected report read; it leaves the level, and the selection moves to its neighbour

|`M`
|mark read all the reports the level shows, by their ids

|`p`, `e`
|the report's project, its epic: their views, without reading it

|`?`
|the report's line of details: its body in full, created and sent, its id
|===

The keys are keys of navigation, the same in every language, like those of the overview. The level follows the runtime's events: a new report is added at its place without moving the selection, a report read elsewhere leaves it.

[#completion]
== A Completion Report

`plan report <plan_id>` prints link:../shared/completion-report.adoc[Completion Report] into the scrollback; in the full-screen view `c` on an ended plan's view opens it as a level (xref:16-plan-view.adoc#ended[The Plan View, An Ended Plan]), and `Esc` goes back.

[source,text]
----
pm › plan report fix-relay
fix-relay · completion report · merged · #214 · today 11:20
  in pm-mcp-clients › relay-hardening                    plan fix-relay
  outcome        merged: the pull request #214 was merged into main
                 https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/pull/214
  reason         —
  ran            2 h 40 min, 08:40 to 11:20 · paused 20 min by you
  deliverables   3 of 3 done
                   done  cursor write order
                   done  a test per transport, websocket and SSE
                   done  fsync of the cursor file
  re-planning    1 · after the base moved (09:55): TASK-5 added
  decisions      2 · Q-003 Rebase onto main (you, the web app) · Q-005 (the plan)
  tokens         ≥ 412 k (≥ 380 k in · ≥ 32 k out) · 2 h 40 min
                 6 workers · 3 models · 3 of 41 tasks not measured
                 plan report fix-relay --tokens
pm ›
----

* The states of the deliverables are words (`done`, `not done`, `dropped`), never a glyph alone. Tokens are counted in the number format of link:../shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#numbers[Texts and Languages, Numbers], never in money.
[source,text]
----
pm › plan report fix-relay --tokens
…
tokens         ≥ 412 k (≥ 380 k in · ≥ 32 k out) · ran 2 h 40 min
  coverage     3 of 41 tasks not measured: their worker was terminated
  by phase     refine      38 k (35 k in · 3 k out)
               outline     96 k (90 k in · 6 k out)
               execute   ≥ 241 k (≥ 222 k in · ≥ 19 k out)   3 not measured
               finalize    37 k (33 k in · 4 k out)
  by role      planner       claude:opus-5.5 · high       128 k (120 k in · 8 k out)
               implementer   claude:sonnet-5.5 · medium   270 k (247 k in · 23 k out)
               reviewer      codex:gpt-5 · low           ≥ 14 k (≥ 13 k in · ≥ 1 k out)   3 not measured
  by model     claude:opus-5.5      128 k (120 k in · 8 k out)
               claude:sonnet-5.5    270 k (247 k in · 23 k out)
               codex:gpt-5        ≥ 14 k (≥ 13 k in · ≥ 1 k out)   3 not measured
  waste        52 k tokens (13 % of measured)
               produced nothing   18 k   2 tasks
               retries            21 k   retryable 1 · stall 1
               remediation        13 k   returned with findings 1
raw components: plan report fix-relay --tokens --components
pm ›
----

* `--tokens` prints the report's section Tokens (link:../shared/completion-report.adoc#tokens[Completion Report, Tokens]) after the report: the total with its split and the wall time, the coverage, the tokens by phase, by role, and by model, and the waste, each a line as below; `--components` adds below each line its raw components as the harnesses reported them (`input 310 k · cache read 62 k · cache write 8 k · output 29 k · reasoning 3 k`, a component a harness does not report `not reported`). Nothing of it is money or a limit.
* `plan <plan_id>` prints the plan as it ended; `plan chat <plan_id>` opens the chat about it (xref:19-actions.adoc#chat-ended[A Chat About Ended Work]).
