[#texts-operator]
== `operator`: Only the Terminal

[#texts-operator-dialogue]
=== The Dialogue

The texts are in the packs link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/operator.en.properties[`operator.en.properties`] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/operator.de.properties[`operator.de.properties`]; the packs are the source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.

[#texts-operator-keys]
=== Keys

One letter per link relation, the mnemonic of its label; the build checks of the packs also check that each is one letter, neither a digit nor `?` (xref:03-dialogues.adoc#keys[Links as Keys and Numbers]).

The texts are in the packs link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/operator.en.properties[`operator.en.properties`] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/operator.de.properties[`operator.de.properties`]; the packs are the source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.

[#texts-operator-entry]
=== The Entry

The texts are in the packs link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/operator.en.properties[`operator.en.properties`] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/operator.de.properties[`operator.de.properties`]; the packs are the source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.

The texts of the console (its badges and notices) are with xref:04-console.adoc#texts-operator-console[The Console]; those of decisions in the console with xref:14-transcripts-decisions.adoc#texts-operator-decisions[Decisions]; those of the paired devices and the machine settings with xref:23-texts-settings.adoc#texts-operator-devices[Texts: Machine Settings and Paired Devices]. Each part stays below 500 lines.
