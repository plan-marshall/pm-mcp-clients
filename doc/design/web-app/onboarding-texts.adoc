Each state is designed for both clients: the web app here (a wireframe), and the terminal dialogue of the console `pm-operator` in link:../terminal-client/README.adoc[Terminal Client] (a transcript of every state, with its texts). The sketches are ASCII sketches of layout and content, not of the visual style. Names, versions, paths, models and figures in them are examples.

[#texts]
== Texts

Every text of the screens above is a key of the message catalogue (link:../shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Texts and Languages, Keys and Packs]). The keys of the areas `common` and `onboard` are shared with the terminal client and listed with their texts in its tables (link:../terminal-client/09-texts-common.adoc#texts[Common, Frame, Automatic Run], link:../terminal-client/10-texts-steps-1-2.adoc[Steps 1 and 2] and link:../terminal-client/11-texts-steps-3-5.adoc[Steps 3 to 5], link:../terminal-client/12-texts-steps-6-7.adoc[Steps 6 and 7]); the web app adds the keys of the area `web` below for what only it shows. A shared label written in lower case (a link label of the terminal, `look again`) is shown on a button with its first letter capitalised (_Look again_, _Suche erneut_); the text is the same.

The keys below of the area `onboard` are new with this design and belong in the terminal client's tables as well, since its dialogues say the same (link:../terminal-client/10-texts-steps-1-2.adoc[Steps 1 and 2], link:../terminal-client/11-texts-steps-3-5.adoc[Steps 3 to 5]); every other `onboard` key named above is defined in its tables. Gone with this design: `onboard.step.harness` (now `onboard.step.harnesses`), `onboard.harness.choose.action_check` (now `.action`), `onboard.harness.blocked.choose_another` (now `.choose_again`), `onboard.catalogue.consent.yes_one` (now `.probes_only`), `onboard.catalogue.scan.models` (now `onboard.catalogue.models.count`), `onboard.catalogue.select.enrolled`, `.found_not_enrolled`, `.enrol`, and the web keys of the icons' consent, now shared.
