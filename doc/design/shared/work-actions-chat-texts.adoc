== Texts

Keys of the area `control`, of link:work-actions.adoc#chat[Chatting with the Planning Role]; the chat's action is `control.plan.chat`, `control.epic.chat` (also on an ended plan or epic), `control.review.chat`, `start.decomposition.chat`, `start.sources.chat`, or `decision.answer.chat` where it is opened. The runtime's message keys of D5 render through these: a narrative line `chat.line.<name>` as `control.chat.line.<name>` (a `chat.line.decision_answered` whose arguments carry no `device` as `control.chat.line.decision_answered_no_device`, and one without a `channel` names no channel rather than reading it as the terminal), an entry `chat.knows.<item>` of the context summary as `control.chat.knows.item` with that `item`, and `queued_revision.applies` (`chat.applies.after_task {task_id}`, `chat.applies.on_start`) as `control.chat.revision_queued` (`applies` `after_task` or `on_start`) and `control.chat.at_boundary` / `control.chat.at_start`; an `applies` key the wire does not name reads as `control.chat.revision_queued_unscheduled`, which names no task. A step of a reply `chat.step.<read|search|check>` renders as `control.chat.step.<read|search|check>`, a failed turn's reason `chat.reason.lost`, `chat.reason.too_long` and `chat.reason.infeasible` as `control.chat.lost`, `control.chat.too_long_reply` and `control.chat.infeasible`, and a refused proposal's finding `chat.finding.<check_failed|changed_since|lapsed|already_queued>` as `control.chat.<check_failed|changed_since|lapsed|already_queued>`. One name per meaning: `{subject}` is the chat's subject (the plan, the epic, the entry, the decision, the ended work), `{plan}` a plan a message is about (the chat's plan, the plan a decision belongs to, an epic's row), so a revision in a decision's chat names its plan, never the decision; `{task_id}` a task's id. An argument that is itself a key — a pause's `{point}`, a decision's or a hold's `{reason}`, an `{outcome}`, a source's flag `{reason}`, a waiver's `{gate}`, a re-planning `{trigger}`, an item's `{disposition}` — is rendered through its own catalogue: `{point}` through the pause points of link:work-actions-texts.adoc[Actions on Plans and Epics, Texts], a decision's `{reason}` through link:decisions-texts.adoc#texts[Decisions, Texts], `{outcome}` through `overview.ended.outcome` (link:plan-overview.adoc#texts[Plan Overview, Texts]), also the `{outcome}` of `control.chat.line.row_failed`, which is the ended plan's outcome that mapped onto the epic's row, never the row's status, `{disposition}` through link:work-actions-review-texts.adoc[Reviews and Holds, Texts], a hold's `{reason}` and a source's flag `{reason}` through their own keys of the same areas; a waiver's `{gate}` and a re-planning `{trigger}` are identifiers and pass through as they are, in code style, never translated. A chat inside a decision adds the keys `decision.chat.*` (link:decisions-texts.adoc#texts[Decisions, Texts]), a chat while starting `start.chat.*` (link:starting-work-texts.adoc#texts[Starting Work, Texts]).

The texts are in the packs
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/control.en.properties[`control.en.properties`] and
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/control.de.properties[`control.de.properties`]; the packs are the
source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.
