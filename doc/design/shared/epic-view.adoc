An epic's view is what the plan view is for a plan (link:plan-view.adoc[Plan View]): the whole epic at a glance, from which the operator reaches any of its plans and acts on the epic. It opens from the epic's row in Running Work (link:plan-overview.adoc[Plan Overview]).

== Layout

  relay-hardening · epic             running · automatic · 2 of 3 at once   ! 3
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [▶ Next plan: relay-reconnect-metrics]  [Automatic: on]  [Decompose again]
  [Findings review · 3 new]  [Watches review · 5]  [Chat]  [Archive]  [Cancel]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

      ✓  relay-reconnect-backoff      shipped · #211                 2 d ago
      ▶  relay-cursor-order           execute · running              40 min

      ○  relay-reconnect-metrics      staged · can start
  ▾ WS-2  Authentication                                   1 running · 1 staged
      ▶  relay-auth-v2                refine · waiting for you   !   12 min
      ○  relay-token-rotation         staged · after relay-auth-v2
      ○  relay-docs                   staged · waits: overlap with relay-auth-v2

    12:05  relay-reconnect-backoff landed; relay-reconnect-metrics re-ordered
    11:55  you paused relay-reconnect-docs

----

* *The head*: the path `project › epic` (`pm-mcp-clients › relay-hardening`), the project one step away: in the web app a link, in the terminal a key (link:../terminal-client/19-actions.adoc#epic-view[Terminal Client, An Epic's View]), opening the project in Running Work (link:plan-overview.adoc#levels[Plan Overview]); the title, the state from one closed list (running, waiting for you, held, landing, ended), whether it starts its plans by itself (`automatic` or `manual`, <<reactions>>), how many plans run of how many may at once, the marker of what needs the operator with its count, and the epic's actions, those of its state (link:work-actions.adoc#epic-actions[An Epic's Actions]: while it lands, _Archive_ waits for the ledger). _Next plan_ names a plan that can start now (here `relay-reconnect-metrics`: `relay-token-rotation` waits for `relay-auth-v2`); with none it is disabled with its reason.
* *Workstreams*: open or closed like a node of the plan view, each with its counts per status; each plan with its status from one closed list (staged, running, pausing, paused, shipped, cancelled, abandoned, held; a paused plan is always paused by the operator and says so; a plan aborted from an integrity pause shows `cancelled` here and keeps its outcome `aborted`, link:work-actions.adoc#epic-told[The Epic Is Told]), what a staged plan waits for (a dependency, a lock, the scope full, an overlap with a running plan: `waits: overlap with relay-auth-v2`, <<reactions>>) or `can start`, and since when. A shipped plan shows its pull request by its number with a link. A staged plan opens to its objective, deliverables, surface, and dependencies; a plan that exists opens its plan view, whose path leads back to this epic in one step (link:plan-view.adoc#way-out[Plan View, Its Project and Its Epic]).
* *Sources, landings, findings, watches*: one line each (findings: how many are new since the last review, how many are open, not decided yet, and how many are kept; watches: how many are open and how many kept), opening to the sources as stored, the landings with their pull requests and the epic's analysis, and the reviews (link:work-actions.adoc#review[Reviewing Findings or Watches]).
* *Events*: the epic's history, newest first: plans started and ended, messages of its plans, the operator's acts (the automatic mode switched among them), decompositions, landings, and what the epic did by itself after a plan ended (<<reactions>>).
* *What waits for the operator* (a decomposition, a held item, a decision) stands first in the head, with a link to it.
* *An ended epic* shows read-only as it ended, with its end (`completed`, `cancelled`, or `released`; link:work-actions.adoc#outcomes[How a Plan Ends]), reason, and end time in the head, its plans with how each ended (a plan aborted from an integrity pause by its outcome `aborted`), without live updates and without actions but _Chat_, a read-only chat about it (link:work-actions.adoc#chat-ended[A Chat About Ended Work]), opened from Ended Work (link:plan-overview.adoc#ended[Ended Work]).

[#reactions]
== What the Epic Does by Itself

An epic manages its plans (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-2[UC-CTL-2], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/06-controlling-work.adoc#change-epic-auto[An Epic Runs by Itself]); the view shows what it did, and never decides anything itself:

* *Automatic or manual*: the head says `automatic` while the epic starts its launchable plans by itself, up to how many may run at once, and `manual` while it starts none and waits for _Next plan_ (link:work-actions.adoc#epic-auto[Automatic: On or Off]). Everything below runs in both modes.
* *Waits: overlap*: a staged plan whose declared surface shares more files with a running plan than the epic allows waits until that plan has ended, and its row says so (`waits: overlap with relay-auth-v2`); `?` in the terminal or the row's detail in the web app names the files in common. It is never started beside that plan, also not by _Next plan_.
* *An overlap found*: two running plans whose changes have come to share files beyond the limit go on; the epic records it, the event names both plans and the files (`relay-cursor-order and relay-docs-site changed 3 files in common`), and a report says so (link:reports.adoc#kinds[Reports]). The one that lands second meets its re-planning when the base moves under it.
* *After a plan landed*: the event names the landing and its downstream judgment (`relay-reconnect-backoff landed; relay-reconnect-metrics re-ordered`); a re-order, or an amended surface or dependency of a staged plan, is applied and shown in the rows; a change that adds, drops, or re-scopes a plan waits for the operator in the decomposition, first in the head as what waits (link:starting-work.adoc#decomposition[The Decomposition]).
* *After a plan ended without landing* (cancelled, abandoned, handed back, released, aborted): the plans that depend on it are held, each with that reason, and the epic asks the operator as every hold does (link:work-actions.adoc#holds[What Holds a Plan]).
* *The next plans*: then, automatic, the epic starts what can start (`started relay-token-store (2 of 3 at once)`); manual, the event names what can start now (`can start now: relay-token-store`), a report says so once per change (link:reports.adoc#kinds[Reports]), and _Next plan_ offers it.
* *Through*: once every plan has shipped or ended, an automatic epic runs its completion review and reports it (`relay-hardening is through: the completion review is ready`); the review opens from the head, and _Archive_ stays the operator's act (link:work-actions.adoc#archive-check[Archiving]). A manual epic reports that it is through (`relay-hardening is through: archiving runs its completion review`) and runs the review when the operator chooses _Archive_, before the archive check.
