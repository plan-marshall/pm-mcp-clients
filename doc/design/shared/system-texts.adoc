[#texts]
== Texts

Keys of the area `system` (link:texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Keys and Packs]). Sizes, durations, and times are arguments formatted by the client in the interface language (`412 MB`, `6 h 12 min`, `today 08:14`); the plans' and epics' states are the texts `overview.state.*` of link:plan-overview.adoc#texts[Plan Overview, Texts]. The terminal's own texts are in link:../terminal-client/25-system.adoc#texts[Terminal Client, System].
