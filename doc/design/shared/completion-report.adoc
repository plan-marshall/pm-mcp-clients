_Relies on: link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-token-usage[Tokens, Not Money] (proposed), link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-reports[Reports to the Operator] (proposed), link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-ended-work[Ended Work] (proposed), link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-plan-history[The Plan's History]_

Status: `DRAFT`. Shared by both clients: link:../terminal-client/24-reports.adoc#completion[Terminal Client, Reports], link:../web-app/README.adoc#structure[Web App].

How a plan went, on one page: the detail of the report of its end (link:reports.adoc#detail[Reports, The Detail]) and one step from the plan as it ended (link:plan-view.adoc#ended[Plan View, An Ended Plan]). The plan view shows the process node by node; the completion report says what came of it. It is a view of its own, read from the archive and the plan's records (its completion record, its re-planning records, its decisions, its usage records); the client computes nothing in it. It is the resource `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/completion`.

[#layout]
== Layout

----
  pm-mcp-clients › relay-hardening › fix-relay
  fix-relay · completion report                     merged · #214 · today 11:20
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Outcome        merged: the pull request #214 was merged into main
  Reason         —
  Ran            2 h 40 min, 08:40 to 11:20 · paused 20 min by you
  Deliverables   3 of 3 done
                   ✓ cursor write order
                   ✓ a test per transport, websocket and SSE
                   ✓ fsync of the cursor file
  Re-planning    1 · after the base moved (09:55): TASK-5 added
  Decisions      2 · Q-003 Rebase onto main (you, web app) · Q-005 (the plan)
  Tokens         ≥ 412 k (≥ 380 k in · ≥ 32 k out) · 2 h 40 min      [Tokens]
                 6 workers · 3 models · 3 of 41 tasks not measured
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Its plan view]   [Chat]
----

* *The head*: the path `project › epic › plan` as in the plan view, each part one step away (link:plan-view.adoc#way-out[Plan View, Its Project and Its Epic]); the title; the outcome (link:work-actions.adoc#outcomes[How a Plan Ends]); the plan's own pull request by its number with a link; when it ended.
* *Outcome and reason*: the outcome in words, with what it means for this plan (merged into which branch; handed back as a pull request, a branch, or changes in the working tree, with what is missing; returned with its planning result); the reason where the operator or the plan gave one, else `—`.
* *Ran*: from its start to its end, and how long it was paused by the operator, where it was.
* *Deliverables*: how many of all are done, each with its state (done `✓`, not done `○`, dropped by a re-planning `–`); a plan that ended before its outline has none and says so (`no deliverables yet: it ended in refine`).
* *Re-planning*: how often the plan was revised, each with its trigger (the base moved, a failed gate, a chat of the operator) and what changed in one line; opening one leads to it in the plan's history.
* *Decisions*: how many were asked, each with its id, the answer, and who answered (you, with the channel and device; the plan, where the decision allowed it to decide; withdrawn); opening one shows it as recorded (link:decisions.adoc#outcomes[Decisions, When It Ends Elsewhere]).
* *Tokens*: the tokens the plan used, with the split and the wall time, the workers, the models, and the coverage; _Tokens_ opens the section of its own, one step away (<<tokens>>). No money is shown.
* *Actions*: _Its plan view_ opens the plan as it ended; _Chat_ opens the read-only chat about it (link:work-actions.adoc#chat-ended[A Chat About Ended Work]). The report has no other action: the archive never changes.

[#tokens]
== Tokens

What the plan used, in tokens and never in money (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-token-usage[Tokens, Not Money]). Every figure is one the runtime derived from the plan's usage records; the client formats it (link:texts-and-languages.adoc#numbers[Texts and Languages, Numbers]) and adds nothing up.

----
  fix-relay · tokens                                merged · #214 · today 11:20
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Total        ≥ 412 k tokens (≥ 380 k in · ≥ 32 k out) · ran 2 h 40 min
  Coverage     3 of 41 tasks not measured: their worker was terminated
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  By phase     refine      38 k (35 k in · 3 k out)
               outline     96 k (90 k in · 6 k out)
               execute   ≥ 241 k (≥ 222 k in · ≥ 19 k out)    3 not measured
               finalize    37 k (33 k in · 4 k out)
  By role      planner       opus-5.5 · high      128 k (120 k in · 8 k out)
               implementer   sonnet-5.5 · medium ≥ 250 k (≥ 228 k in · ≥ 22 k out)
               reviewer      sonnet-5.5 · low      34 k (32 k in · 2 k out)
  By model     claude:opus-5.5      128 k (120 k in · 8 k out)
               claude:sonnet-5.5    270 k (247 k in · 23 k out)
               codex:gpt-5        ≥ 14 k (≥ 13 k in · ≥ 1 k out)       3 not measured
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Waste        52 k tokens (13 %)
               produced nothing      18 k   2 tasks
               retries               21 k   retryable 1 · stall 1
               remediation           13 k   returned with findings 1
----

* *Total*: the plan's tokens with the split, `tokens = in + out` (_in_: input, cache-read, and cache-write tokens; _out_: output and reasoning tokens), and the wall time from its start to its end, as _Ran_ above.
* *Coverage*: how many of the plan's tasks were measured, and for those that were not, why, by the closed list of the records (`not_reported_by_harness`, `output_unparsed`, `session_task`, `terminated`), `common.tokens.coverage`. A figure the harness's profile declares not applicable (no cache write for its harness) counts as `0` and leaves the task measured. Any total over a task not measured is a floor, shown with `≥`, never as exact; with every task measured the line says `41 of 41 tasks measured`.
* *By phase*: one line per phase the plan ran, in its order; a phase whose tasks were not all measured names how many.
* *By role*: one line per role, with what the role pinned (harness, model, effort), as the plan's records name them.
* *By model*: one line per `<harness>:<model>`.
* *Raw components*, one step from any line of _Total_, _By phase_, _By role_, or _By model_: the figures as the harnesses reported them, summed by the runtime for that line: input, cache read, cache write, output, reasoning, each `not reported` where a harness of the line does not report it and `0` where its profile declares it not applicable. The unified figure stays the one of the line; the components explain it and are never added up by the client.
* *Waste*: the tokens that produced nothing, with their share of the total: the tasks of spend class `no_product` (a task that ended without a product), and the retries and the remediation of the plan, each by its cause class (`retryable`, `stall`, `terminal_error`, `returned_with_findings`, …) with how often. Nothing is waste that the records do not class as such.
* *Only usage*: no budget, no limit, no projection, and no money. A re-planning or a waiver of the plan is in _Re-planning_ above and in the plan's history as an event, never as a figure of this section.
* Each line of _By phase_ leads to that phase in the plan view as it ended (link:plan-view.adoc#ended[Plan View, An Ended Plan]); `Esc` or _Back_ returns to the report.

[#reached]
== Where It Is Reached

* From the report of the plan's end: _Show the details_, which reads the report (link:reports.adoc#read[Reports, Reading a Report]).
* From the plan as it ended: the head of the ended plan view offers _Completion report_ (link:plan-view.adoc#ended[An Ended Plan]); from the completion report, _Its plan view_ goes back.
* From a row of Ended Work: a plan's row opens its plan view as before (link:plan-overview.adoc#ended[Ended Work]); its completion report is one step from there.
* Only a plan that has ended has one. An epic's counterpart is its completion review, in its view (link:epic-view.adoc#reactions[Epic View, What the Epic Does by Itself]).
