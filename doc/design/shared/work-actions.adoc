Status: `DRAFT`. Shared by both clients: link:../terminal-client/19-actions.adoc[Terminal Client, Actions], link:../web-app/README.adoc[Web App].

A plan and an epic carry their actions wherever they are shown: their row in Running Work (link:plan-overview.adoc[Plan Overview]), the head of the plan view (link:plan-view.adoc[Plan View]), and an epic's level of Running Work. The actions are those of the current state only; the client takes them from the runtime and never decides itself what applies.

[#plan-actions]
== A Plan's Actions

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|State |Actions |Shown as

|running
|Pause, Cancel
|`running`; _Pause_ first

|idle: paused
|Start, Abandon
|`paused` since when, by the operator

|idle: emitted
|Start, Abandon
|`not started` since when, by its epic

|ended
|none
|its outcome; the plan view only
|===

* *Pause* and *Start* act at once, without a confirmation: both are undone by the other. A paused plan's row says what it still holds (`a job finishes: build · 2 min`).
* *Cancel* and *Abandon* confirm first (<<confirm-end>>).
* *A plan of an epic* says on every action which epic is told (`fix-relay-epic is told`), and after it, that it was (<<epic-told>>).
* An action that applies but cannot run now is disabled with its reason (`the runtime is reconnecting`).

[#confirm-end]
=== Ending a Plan

----
  Cancel fix-relay?
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  What ends      the running work: 1 job, 2 tasks
  Pull request   #214 is open   (●) Close it, with the reason   ( ) Leave it open
  Its epic       relay-hardening is told
  Reason         [the approach is replaced by plan relay-v2              ]
  Afterwards     the plan is archived as cancelled; its branch stays
                                                  [Keep it running]  [Cancel the plan]
----

* The reason is required; it goes to the plan's archive, to the epic, and to the pull request's closing comment.
* _Abandon_ is the same dialogue for an idle plan, without _What ends_.

[#epic-told]
=== The Epic Is Told

After an action on a plan of an epic, the row of the plan shows for a moment `relay-hardening told`, and the epic's level shows the act among its events. What the epic does with it comes later, as any epic event, and a decision it raises reaches the operator as every decision does (link:decisions.adoc[Decisions]).

[#epic-actions]
== An Epic's Actions

----
  relay-hardening · epic                         3 running · 1 idle · 4 staged
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [▶ Next plan: relay-reconnect-backoff]   [Findings review · 3 new]
  [Watches review · 5]   [Archive]   [Cancel]
----

* *Next plan* names the plan it will start: the next launchable row in the queue's order. With none launchable it is disabled and says why per row (`relay-auth-v2 waits for relay-reconnect-backoff`, `relay-docs: the surface overlaps a running plan`); with the epic's scope full it offers _Start it anyway: run 4 at once_.
* *Findings review* and *Watches review* carry their counts; new findings since the last review are also a `needs you` of the epic in Running Work.
* *Archive* runs the check first (<<archive-check>>).
* *Cancel* confirms with the choice for its plans (<<cancel-epic>>).

[#archive-check]
=== Archiving: Is It Through?

----
  Archive relay-hardening?
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓  no plan running or idle
    ✓  every plan shipped or ended                       11 shipped · 1 cancelled
    ✗  open findings                                     2   [Review the findings]
    ✓  the ledger is landed
    ✓  the completion review is done
    ·  watches still open                                1   go into the archive
                                                                   [Not yet]
----

Each line is a check of the runtime; a failing one leads to what is open. _Archive the epic_ is offered only when every check passes.

[#cancel-epic]
=== Cancelling an Epic

----
  Cancel relay-hardening?
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Its plans      3 running · 1 idle
                 (●) Cancel them too   ( ) Let them run on without the epic
  Reason         [                                                        ]
  Afterwards     the epic is archived as aborted
                                                  [Keep it]  [Cancel the epic]
----

[#review]
== Reviewing Findings or Watches

The review goes through the items of one register, one at a time, open ones first; the list beside it shows each item's disposition so far.

----
  relay-hardening · findings review                               3 of 7 decided
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  F-0004  Reconnect loses the last event                                   open
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Statement   After a reconnect the relay resends from the stored cursor, but
              the cursor is written after the event is delivered.
  Rules       a fix must hold for every transport; covered by a test that
              drops the connection between delivery and cursor write
  Origin      landing of relay-reconnect (#211) · 2 days ago
  Proposal    Promote to a plan: it needs a change across two modules
              (the planning role)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Keep ▾ as defect | as watch]  [Drop]  [Promote to a plan]  [Edit]  [Talk]
                                       [Accept the remaining 4 proposals]
----

* *Keep* as a defect (a finding) or as a watch: the item may change its register.
* *Drop* asks for the reason in one line.
* *Promote to a plan* shows the planning role at work, then its draft of the staged plan (objective, deliverables, surface, dependencies) with _Stage it_, _Edit_, and _Back_.
* *Edit* asks what to adapt or add, in the operator's words; the planning role rewrites the statement and the rules, and the read-back shows old against new with _Accept_, _Instruct again_, and _Discard_.
* *Talk* opens a conversation with the planning role about this item or the whole register; each change it proposes is shown as a read-back, and nothing changes without _Accept_.
* *Accept the remaining proposals* lists them all once for one confirmation.
* *An item from outside the machine* (a finding from a review comment) carries `untrusted`, and its text is shown as text, never rendered.
* *Finish* writes the decisions to the epic's ledger; leaving before keeps them, and the review opens where it stopped.
