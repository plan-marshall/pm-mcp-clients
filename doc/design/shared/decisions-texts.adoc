== Texts

The texts both clients use for decisions, keys of the area `decision` (link:texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Keys and Packs]); the keys `decision.chat.*` and `decision.answer.chat` are those of link:decisions.adoc#chat[A Chat Before Answering]. The German `decision.project.fallback` is the dative "dem Projekt", because every template puts `{project}` after "von" or "in".

The texts are in the packs
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/decisions.en.properties[`decisions.en.properties`] and
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/decisions.de.properties[`decisions.de.properties`]; the packs are the
source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.
