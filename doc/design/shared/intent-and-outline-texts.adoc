[#texts]
== Texts

Keys of the area `intent` (link:texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Keys and Packs]). A record's own text (a goal, a criterion, a reason, a path) is never a key: it is shown as it stands, as text.

The texts are in the packs
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/intent.en.properties[`intent.en.properties`] and
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/intent.de.properties[`intent.de.properties`]; the packs are the source
of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.

* *The head's counts*: the head joins `intent.head.criteria` and one `intent.head.count` per verdict that has criteria, with ` · `; `{state}` is the verdict's word `state.criterion.<value>` (<<states>>), so a verdict has one word everywhere. Before the review the line is `state.criterion.not_reviewed`.
* *The head's title*: `{plan}` in `intent.head.title` is the plan's id, as in the sketch; its title is the plan view's head, one step away.
* *File intents*: the wire values `read`, `write-new`, `write-replace`, `write-conditional` and `delete` take the keys `intent.outline.file.<value>` with `-` written as `_`.
* *Sources* at the top of the epic's tab take `overview.epic.sources` (link:epic-view.adoc#texts[Epic View, Texts]).

[#states]
== Verdicts

The verdicts are display words of a server value, and of the absence of one for `not_reviewed`, the family `state.<kind>.<value>` of the area `common` (link:texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Keys and Packs]); a `StatusLabel` shows them (link:../web-app/components-tags.adoc#status-label[Web App, Tags and Kinds, StatusLabel]), and the head line's `not reviewed yet` is the same key.

The texts are in the packs
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/common.en.properties[`common.en.properties`] and
link:../../../pm-api/src/main/resources/messages/common.de.properties[`common.de.properties`]; the packs are the
source of these texts, and this design does not repeat them.
