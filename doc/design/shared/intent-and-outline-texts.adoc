* *The head's counts*: the head joins `intent.head.criteria` and one `intent.head.count` per verdict that has criteria, with ` · `; `{state}` is the verdict's word `state.criterion.<value>` (<<states>>), so a verdict has one word everywhere. Before the review the line is `state.criterion.not_reviewed`.
* *The head's title*: `{plan}` in `intent.head.title` is the plan's id, as in the sketch; its title is the plan view's head, one step away.
* *The chart's title*: `intent.oversize.title` while the runtime serves a proposed multiple, `intent.oversize.title_current` without one.
* *File intents*: the wire values `read`, `write-new`, `write-replace`, `write-conditional` and `delete` take the keys `intent.outline.file.<value>` with `-` written as `_`.
* *Sources* at the top of the epic's tab take `overview.epic.sources` (link:epic-view.adoc#texts[Epic View, Texts]).

== Verdicts
