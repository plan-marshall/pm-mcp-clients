[#texts]
== Texts

Keys of the area `system` (link:texts-and-languages.adoc#keys[Keys and Packs]). Sizes, dates, and times are arguments formatted by the client in the interface language; `{what}` is `system.cleanup.summary.what` with the counts concerned, the outcomes are `overview.ended.outcome` and `overview.ended.epic_end` (link:plan-overview.adoc#texts[Plan Overview, Texts]), and who decided is `system.by` (link:system-texts.adoc#texts-lifecycle[System, Texts]). The report's subject is `report.subject.system_cleanup_due` (link:reports.adoc#texts[Reports, Texts]); the terminal's own texts are in link:../terminal-client/26-cleanup.adoc#texts[Terminal Client, Cleanup].
