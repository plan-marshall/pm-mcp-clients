== Overview

This document is the index of the sections of the Job Runtime Specification that the launcher `pm-exec` implements
alone: the mechanisms it applies on Linux beside the Landlock sets (the ABI floor, the Landlock scopes, the system
call filter, a job without network), the macOS sandbox profile, and its parent watch when the runtime is lost. The
family is split across two repositories, at a heading: the runtime's sections stay in `pm-mcp-core`
(link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/job-runtime.adoc[the family index]),
among them the sections both implement, the runtime composing and recording and the launcher applying: the process
group and the launch report, the termination protocol, the Linux filesystem confinement with its read and write sets
and its socket list, the worker read set, the egress proxy, the probe jobs and the confinement self-check. The
family's `Overview`, `Traceability`, `Status` and the runtime's `Modules` rows stay with that index; this document
lists the parts of this repository alone. The requirements stay in `plan-marshall-documentation`
(link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc[Requirements Document]); what implements and verifies
each of them is recorded in link:../Traceability.adoc[Traceability].
