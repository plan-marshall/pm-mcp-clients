== Tasks

. `pm-operator drain` signals the runtime process recorded in `state/runtime.json`, does nothing when none is recorded, and waits at most the bound defined with the drain timeout; it sends no request to the runtime's API.
  Anchor: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#cli-without-runtime[CLI, Coordination & Security § Without the Runtime]; link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#drain-timeout[Timeouts & Budgets § Drain Timeout]; link:../design/terminal-client/25-system.adoc[Terminal Client, System] (`drain` asks nothing and cannot be withdrawn).
  Proof: `DrainCommandTest` with a fixture process recorded in `state/runtime.json`: the signal reaches it, the command returns once it exits and at the bound when it does not; no record means no signal and exit `0`; no HTTP request is sent.
. `--withdraw` leaves `drain`; `runtime stop --withdraw` withdraws a drain the operator asked with `DELETE /api/v1/operator/runtime/stop`, and its refusals print the design's lines.
  Anchor: link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#withdraw-drain[Runtime Lifecycle § Drain, Stop & Upgrade]; link:../design/terminal-client/25-system.adoc#t-stop[Terminal Client, System, Stopping]; link:../design/terminal-client/01-commands.adoc#command-tree[Terminal Client, The Verbs].
  Proof: `DrainCommandTest` refuses `--withdraw` as a usage error (exit `2`); the terminal's tests against the mock runtime withdraw an operator's drain, are refused for a drain of a signal (`operator.system.signal_drain`) and too late (`system.stopping.too_late`).
