== What

* `pm-relay`: `stdout` hygiene, relay termination, and the relay rules at the process level against the stub daemon.
* `pm-operator`: the operator checks and the runtime-free verbs at the process level.
* What needs the real daemon (restart in the generation, `hello` from a second connection) is link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/integration-layers.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `integration-layers`].
