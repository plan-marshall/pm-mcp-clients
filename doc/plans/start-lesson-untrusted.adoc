== Tasks

. Design the marked lesson in link:../design/shared/starting-work.adoc#lesson[Starting Work, A Lesson] and link:../design/terminal-client/18-starting.adoc#lesson[Terminal Client, A Lesson], as an issue's flagged and unscreened waits; the operator accepts the design before the build.
. The mark and the three screening states of `source-lesson` in the start contract; the mock runtime offers a marked lesson whose screening is clean, flagged, and failed.
  Anchor: link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-catalogue-source[State Catalogue § Source Units]; link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-record[Store Schemas § Lesson Record].
  Proof: contract tests against the scenarios.
. Both clients choose a marked lesson and show its mark and screening.
  Anchor: link:../design/shared/starting-work.adoc#lesson[Starting Work, A Lesson].
  Proof: both clients pass through a clean, a flagged, and a failed screening against the mock runtime in their tests, and an unmarked lesson is stored without a screening.
