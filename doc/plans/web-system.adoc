== What

* *Design first*: no web design exists for these views. The package lays them out in the design system of `web-design` from the shared design, and has the operator accept the layout before building it: the entry _System_ in the frame beside the machine settings, the view as a route of its own (`/system`), the confirmations as dialogues over it, the progress line of a drain or an upgrade in the frame, the page of a stopped runtime (link:../design/web-app/README.adoc#structure[Web App, Structure]).
* The system view with its five parts, live from `GET /api/v1/events`, each figure a link to what it counts, the window, the grouping, and per project remembered per browser; a figure unavailable with its reason, never `0`.
* The models in tokens, never money: the total with its split per model or role, a floor with the calls not measured, the raw components in the line's detail (link:../design/shared/system.adoc#models[System, The Models]), in link:../design/shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#numbers[the number format of tokens].
* Stop, restart, and upgrade from a paired device, each with its confirmation and its choice of when; the drain's line with _Stop now_ and _Keep it running_ on every device; the stopped page with the command to copy, connecting again by itself; the restart's reconnect; the check behind its consent screen; the upgrade's check list, its withdraw, its failures; loading the app again after an upgrade, waiting where a field holds unsent text.
* Every view at desktop and phone width, light and dark; texts from the catalogue only (area `system`).
