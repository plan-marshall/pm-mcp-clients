== What

* *Design first*: no web design exists for these views. The package lays them out in the design system of `web-design` from the shared designs, and has the operator accept the layout before building it: the entry _Reports_ in the frame of the app with the count of unread reports, the list as a route of its own (`/reports`), the arrival line at the top of the first view, the quiet notice in the frame, the completion report as a view of an ended plan (link:../design/web-app/README.adoc#structure[Web App, Structure]).
* The entry with its count while there are unread reports; on opening the app or after pairing, with unread reports, the line `5 unread reports since …` with _Show them_, one step to the list.
* The list of unread reports grouped by project, epic, and plan, newest first, paged, with from (each part a link that reads nothing), subject, body, created and sent, _Show the details_, _Mark read_, and _Mark all shown read_ sending the ids shown; live from `GET /api/v1/reports/events`: a new report in its place, a report read in another client or browser leaving the list.
* A new report while the app is open as a quiet notice: a polite live region, no sound, never a browser notification, the tab title unchanged, coalesced.
* A report's detail opened in place, reading it: the completion report, the plan view, the epic view, the project, the start at its sources; a subject that has ended as it ended, one the cleanup deleted with the detail disabled; the completion report also from an ended plan's view.
* The completion report's section Tokens (link:../design/shared/completion-report.adoc#tokens[Completion Report, Tokens]): the total with its split and the wall time, the coverage, by phase, by role, by model, the waste, each line's raw components one step away, a floor marked `≥`, in link:../design/shared/texts-and-languages.adoc#numbers[the number format of tokens]; no money and no limit anywhere.
* Every view at desktop and phone width, light and dark; texts from the catalogue only (area `report`).
