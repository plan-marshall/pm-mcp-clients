== What

The package implements in `pm-api` and `pm-mock-runtime` the client side of the specification sections listed under _Trace_; the engine side is `pm-mcp-core`'s `process-designer`. A statement of them that the implementation proves wrong is corrected in `plan-marshall-documentation` in the same plan (link:README.adoc#how[How to Work a Package]).

* The installed units as a record of `pm-api`, in two scopes: a project's, `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/workflows`, and the machine's, `GET /api/v1/workflows` (the machine level's units with the compiled defaults, no project needed): each unit with its name, its frontmatter (display name, description, type, entries and exits with their descriptions, author, modification date, release, signature present or not), its group (project-owned, axis variant, recipe, compiled only), every level that has it (`project`, `machine`, `default`; the machine scope has no `project`) with its text, revision, and digest, and the level in force.
* The catalogue of building blocks, in the same two scopes: `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/workflows/catalogue` (as the project resolves it) and `GET /api/v1/workflows/catalogue` (the compiled and machine-level units, the machine's role set). The record: its `scope` (`machine`, or `project` with the project id), the runtime's `version`, its `digest`, and its blocks, each with its `kind` (a closed enumeration: `primitive`, `guard`, `link_relation`, `condition`, `sub_workflow`, `role`, `parameter`, `keyword`), its name, a one-line summary, the text it inserts, what it needs, and where it may be used: a primitive with the clauses that take it (`do`, `auto`, `job`, `guard`), its parameter record and its outcomes; a link relation with its forms and its default form; a condition kind with its operands; a sub-workflow (a phase among them) with its parameters, outcomes, and whether it is compiled only; a role with its class; a declarable parameter with its unit and compiled bounds; a keyword or clause with its template; and, where a unit's kind cannot use the block, the reason. The catalogue is the runtime's, generated from the registries its validation uses; the client keeps no list of its own and reloads it when the digest it holds differs from the one the units' record or the event of a placing names. Every placing and every removal answers, and announces as an event, the resulting catalogue digest of each scope it changes (the machine's, and each project's whose resolution it changes) and the changed units record; the designer reloads the catalogue and the units when a digest differs from the one it holds. A scenario removes a machine unit that a project resolved and shows the reload.
* All of it on the web listener: the web app calls these endpoints on the runtime's own `/api/v1/` with its device secret, with no CLI in between; none is `cli_only` (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/03-web-server.adoc#web-proxy[API Access & Device Secret]).
* The validation, `POST /api/v1/workflows/validate` with the text, the unit's kind and name, and the project it is checked against, or the machine as target with no project (composed against every enrolled project the unit would reach, each finding and composed process naming its project; with none enrolled the composition not run): the stages (parse, static rules, compiled invariants, composition) each passed, failed, or not run; the findings with their rule, line, column, and message; the parsed structure of the unit wherever the text parses (the designer refreshes its structure from it after a pause in typing, link:../../design/web-app/process-designer.adoc#editor[Process Designer, The Text and the Structure]); and, when valid, the composed process in the representation of a plan's process (`process-contract`), every node planned. Nothing is started or written.
* Placing and removing as operator acts of the web channel, without an audit record: `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/workflows/{unit}` (machine level, `<PM_MCP_BASE>/workflows/<unit>.pmwf`) and `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/workflows/{unit}` (project level, `.marshall/config/<unit>.pmwf`, a recipe's `.marshall/recipes/<id>.pmwf`, a short landing; a machine recipe's `<PM_MCP_BASE>/recipes/<id>.pmwf`; under `landing.files` written and left uncommitted in the operator's checkout); a recipe whose key a built-in or domain recipe has is refused with its reason; placing or removing at machine level validates against every enrolled project the unit would reach and is refused while any fails, the findings naming each project; a project-level unit takes effect for a plan when its checkout or worktree has it (new plans once it landed on the base branch, running plans through `config_drift` at their next sync with the base); the request carries the digest of the unit it replaces and is refused with `conflict` and the current unit when it changed meanwhile; placing validates again and is refused with the findings; a compiled-only unit is refused; a unit name outside the canonical unit-name grammar or the designable names, or one whose path would leave its target directory, is refused with `unit_name_invalid` (`400`) on every machine and project route; the result of a project placing as states of its landing (written, committed, landed, failed with its reason); the events of a unit placed, landed, and removed. Download is the client's and has no request.
* The texts of the area `designer` and the key `operator.help.designer` added to the catalogue of `message-catalogue` (link:../../design/web-app/process-designer-texts.adoc#texts[Process Designer, Texts]).
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: a project with units at every level and a compiled-only one; the machine's units with no project enrolled; the catalogue of the machine scope and of a project, with a block of every kind and a block a unit cannot use; the catalogue's digest changing after a placing and after an upgrade, the client reloading it; a machine-level unit designed with no project chosen and validated against every enrolled project at placing; a valid unit with its composed process and the composition leaving phases out; a finding at each stage (a parse error with its line, a static rule, a compiled invariant, a composition that fails against the project) and the stages after it not run; placing on the machine with projects that keep their own copy; placing on the machine refused by a finding in one of the projects it reaches; removing the machine's copy refused the same way; a recipe placed in its project and on the machine, a project recipe of the same key taking its place, a key of a delivered recipe refused; a unit without a frontmatter, a description not below its element, an author `plan-marshall`, and a changed interface of a replacing unit, each a finding; placing in a project landed, under `landing.files`, and with a failed landing tried again; a conflict because the unit changed meanwhile; a placing refused by its re-validation; a compiled-only unit refused; a unit name with `..`, a separator, or an absolute path refused with `unit_name_invalid`; removing at each level, falling back to the next; scenario names `designer-<case>`.

== Done When

Every record and event has a round-trip test of its codec, and the stages, groups, levels, scopes, and block kinds are enumerations a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
