== What

The package implements in `pm-api` and `pm-mock-runtime` the specification sections listed under _Trace_. A statement of them that the implementation proves wrong is corrected in `plan-marshall-documentation` in the same plan (link:README.adoc#how[How to Work a Package]).

* *The decision*: the decision with its parts, options, support, recommendation, revision, and evidence, addressed as `<scope_id>/<question_id>` below its scope (a plan, an epic, an entry, a project's workspace scope, the machine); the states `decision.open` to `decision.closed` and `decision.withdrawn` (`decision.announce`, `.answer`, `.interpret`, `.read-back`, `.clarify` with its bound, `.chat`, `.withdraw`, `.confirm`, `.record`); the answer, the interpretation (`option`, `option_with_condition`, `own_option`, `delegation`, or unclear, which is never confirmed), and the read-back; the channel `cli` with its device; the refusals `invalid_answer`, `dependency_unanswered`, `conflict`, and `already_answered`.
* *None fits and later*: _none fits_ closing the decision withdrawn and the decision asked anew naming the one it replaces, offered for a decision a role asked, and for one no role asked only where a quorum is declared, otherwise not offered; _later_ leaving it open.
* *A waiver request* answered by `waive` or `decline` (outcome `declined`).
* *The open decisions* across scopes (`GET /api/v1/decisions`), newest first with `delivered_at`, a project's workspace scope (a question of its setup) among them, its subject the setup, blocking the setup only; the events `decision.opened`, `decision.changed`, `decision.answered`, `decision.closed`.
* *The chat inside a decision* (represented as in `control-contract`): _Chat_ as a link of `decision.answer` with its availability and the reason when disabled, none on a waiver request; the decision as the chat's scope and its routed role (the asking task's role, or `replan`/`decompose` for a decision a server rule raised or a quorum escalated; for a question the setup's workflow raised itself none, _Chat_ disabled with that reason); a proposed answer handed to `decision.interpret` as the operator's own words; a revision that leaves the decision open or closes it as withdrawn with its reason; the decision's record marking an answer that followed a chat, with the transcript kept with the decision; the chat ended by an answer in another channel.
* *The scenarios of the mock runtime* (`decision-<case>`): none fits with the decision asked anew, none fits not offered for a server rule's decision without a quorum, later, a waiver declined, own words read back, an unclear interpretation clarified and its bound reached, an answer in the other client first; a question asked back in a chat and the decision answered afterwards, a proposed answer taken through the read-back, a revision that withdraws the decision and one that leaves it open, a chat ended by an answer in the web app, Chat disabled while the role does not qualify, a decision of a project's setup with its chat routed to the asking task's role, and one the setup's workflow raised, its Chat disabled with the reason.

== Done When

Every record and event type has a round-trip test of its codec, and every closed list (states, interpretation results, closures, refusals) is an enumeration a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
