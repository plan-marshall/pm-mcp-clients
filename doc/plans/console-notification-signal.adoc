== Tasks

. The console chooses its signal from the environment by the table of the design: the bell, OSC 9, OSC 99 or OSC 777, read once at start; today it writes OSC 9 to every terminal and never rings the bell, so the operator of a terminal that does not show OSC 9 (Terminal.app, a JetBrains IDE, tmux without passthrough) is told nothing.
  Anchor: link:../design/terminal-client/04-console.adoc#notifications[The Console, Notifications].
  Proof: a unit test per row of the table, with the environment of the row (among them `TERM_PROGRAM=ghostty` together with `TERMINAL_EMULATOR=JetBrains-JediTerm`, which rings the bell), asserts the one signal written and that no other is; `console.notify=false` writes none.
. The withdrawal of an answered question's notification (`OSC 99` with its id) is sent only where the signal is OSC 99.
  Anchor: link:../design/terminal-client/04-console.adoc#answered-elsewhere[The Console, Answered Elsewhere].
  Proof: a unit test sees the withdrawal under kitty and nothing under every other signal.
