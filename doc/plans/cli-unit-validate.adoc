== Tasks

. Design the two verbs and their texts in link:../design/terminal-client/01-commands.adoc#command-tree[Terminal Client, The Verbs] (the bullet _No designer_ changes: the terminal validates and lists, and still designs nothing); the operator accepts the design before the build.
. A verb that validates a unit file, for the machine or a project, through the validation endpoint of `designer-contract`, readable and machine-readable, exit code non-zero on findings.
  Anchor: link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#process-designer[Workflow DSL § Designing a Process] (items 1 to 3).
  Proof: the terminal's tests against the mock runtime with a valid unit, a unit with findings (exit code and machine-readable form included), and an unreachable runtime.
. A verb that lists the catalogue of the machine or a project, filterable by kind and name.
  Anchor: link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#process-designer[Workflow DSL § Designing a Process] (item 8).
  Proof: the terminal's tests against the mock runtime, the listing in both forms.
