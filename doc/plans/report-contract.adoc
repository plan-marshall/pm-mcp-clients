== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the client side of the change Reports to the Operator into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]), once the use case is agreed, beside what `pm-mcp-core`'s `operator-reports` writes of the runtime's side, and opens that pull request beside its own.
* The report as a record of `pm-api`: its id (`rep-NNNN`), `from` (the project, and the epic and the plan where there are, each with its link), its `kind` from the closed catalogue (`plan.started`, `plan.ended`, `plan.revised`, `plan.hold_released`, `epic.launchable`, `epic.waits_overlap`, `epic.overlap_found`, `epic.recut_applied`, `epic.through`, `epic.ended`, `project.onboarded`, `project.released`, `source.flagged`, `decision.closed_elsewhere`) as an enumeration, the parameters of its subject, its body, `created_at`, `sent_at`, `read_at` with the channel and device that read it, and its `detail` link where it has one; the hypermedia representation with the links `detail` and `read`.
* The requests: `GET /api/v1/reports?state=unread` (paged, newest first, with the count and the time of the oldest unread report), `POST /api/v1/reports/{report_id}/read`, `POST /api/v1/reports/read` with the ids shown, an already read report answered as read; the stream `GET /api/v1/reports/events` (a report written, reports read, with the channel and device); the states of UC-PLN-6 (`reports.list`, `reports.choose`, `reports.detail`, `reports.read`, `done`).
* The completion report of an ended plan, `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/completion`: outcome, reason, pull request, start, end, time paused, deliverables with their states, re-planning records, decisions with who answered; refused for a plan that has not ended.
* The completion report's tokens (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-token-usage[Tokens, Not Money], the core's `token-usage`): the unified total with its split (`in`: input, cache read, cache write; `out`: output, reasoning) and the wall time; the same by phase, by role (with its harness, model, and effort), and by model; each with its raw components (input, cache read, cache write, output, reasoning; `not_reported` or `not_applicable` per component) and the count of tasks not measured; the waste (spend class `no_product`, and the retries and remediation by cause class); the coverage with the reasons of the closed list; a figure over a task not measured marked as a floor. Every figure comes from the runtime; no money, no budget, no projection, and no limit is part of the record, and a re-planning or a waiver stays a record of the plan's history.
* The texts of the area `report` added to the catalogue of `message-catalogue`: the subjects of every kind, the list, the arrival line, the notice, the completion report (link:../design/shared/reports.adoc#texts[Reports, Texts], link:../design/shared/completion-report.adoc#texts[Completion Report, Texts]); a test that every kind of the enumeration has its subject in both packs.
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: none unread; reports of every kind across two projects, an epic, and a plan of no epic, more than one page; a report arriving while a client is open, and several at once; one marked read, all shown marked read while a new one arrives (it stays unread); a report read in the other client; a report whose detail is read with it; a report whose subject has ended since (its detail as it ended) and one whose subject is no longer kept; a report sent after a restart (`sent_at` later than `created_at`); a completion report for every outcome, one without deliverables; its tokens fully measured, partly measured (a floor with its coverage), with a figure not applicable to a harness (counted `0`, measured), with nothing measured, and with waste of each kind; scenario names `reports-<case>`.

== Done When

The specification of the change is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; every record and event has a round-trip test of its codec, and the catalogue of kinds is an enumeration a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
