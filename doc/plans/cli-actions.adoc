== What

* `plan pause|start|cancel|abandon|chat` (the pause with its point; `plan cancel` on an integrity pause as its abort) and `config <aspect> --plan` with its refusals and a value changed meanwhile (`conflict`, applied again or kept; UC-CTL-5) and `epic next|decompose|archive|cancel|findings|watches|chat` as direct verbs, `epic decompose` opening the decomposition of `cli-start`, with their confirmations and refusals; the actions below a row of the full-screen view (`a`); the actions per state of a plan (waiting and held too) and of an epic (landing too), `epic next` raising the scope or leaving it; `epic auto <epic_id> on|off` and `u` in the menu, a row waiting for an overlap; the archive check's lines with what it carries; the review as a dialogue with keep as a finding or to the watches, the edit of a drafted plan, read-back, and chat; the chat dialogue of `cli-start` opened from the verbs, the menu (`h`), and the review, with the scope of each and a revision applied at the next task boundary; the chat about ended work, `plan chat` and `epic chat` on an ended plan or epic and `a` on ended work and the ended views offering _Chat…_ alone, its follow-up plan opening `plan add` at its sources with the description stored.
* `epic <epic_id>` as the epic's view in the full-screen view and as a tree (UC-PLN-5: the path project › epic, its mode in the head, its reactions among the events, `p` to the project, the tree's line with `status --project`; workstreams and plans with their status, sources, landings, registers, the epic's tokens with their split per plan and its own (a floor where tasks were not measured, never money), events); what holds a plan, the epic, or a landing, with the acts that resolve it, and after an act the affected plan, epic, or landing going on or the hold shown again with what still holds (UC-CTL-4).
