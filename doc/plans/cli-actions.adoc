== What

* `plan pause|start|cancel|abandon` (the pause with its point) and `epic next|decompose|archive|cancel|findings|watches` as direct verbs, `epic decompose` opening the decomposition of `cli-start`, with their confirmations and refusals; the actions below a row of the full-screen view (`a`); the archive check's lines; the review as a dialogue with read-back and talk.
* `epic <epic_id>` as the epic's view in the full-screen view and as a tree (UC-PLN-5: workstreams and plans with their status, sources, landings, registers, events); what holds a plan, the epic, or a landing, with the acts that resolve it (UC-CTL-4).
