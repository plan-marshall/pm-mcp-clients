== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the client side of the changes of Controlling Work into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]), once the use cases are agreed, and opens that pull request beside its own.
* The actions a plan or an epic offers in its state, each with its availability and the reason when disabled; the requests of each action; the confirmation of an end (what ends, the pull request, the reason); the archive check's lines; the cancel of an epic with the choice for its plans.
* The decomposition: the draft (workstreams, plans with objective, deliverables, surface, dependencies, order), the checks per plan, the changes of a later run, and its states with read-back and talk.
* The epic as a resource: its header, workstreams and rows with status and what each waits for, sources, landings, registers, its history.
* The holds and their acts: each hold's kind, cause, attempts, and the acts that resolve it.
* The pause with its point (now, after the task, after the phase) and the states pausing and paused.
* The review: an item (id, statement, rules, origin, trust, state, proposal), the dispositions, the planning role's draft, rewrite, and talk as states of the review, the read-back.
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: a plan paused now, after the task, and after the phase, a pause withdrawn while pausing, a plan started again, cancelled with its pull request closed, abandoned while paused; a first decomposition agreed after an edit and a failing check, a later run with changed, new, and dropped plans; each of them in an epic, the epic told; next plan started, none launchable, the scope full; an archive check failing on open findings and passing; an epic cancelled with and without its plans; a findings review with every disposition, an edit read back and discarded, a talk, the remaining proposals accepted, a review left and continued; scenario names `control-<case>`.
