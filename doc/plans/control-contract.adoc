== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the client side of the changes of Controlling Work into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]), once the use cases are agreed, and opens that pull request beside its own.
* The actions a plan or an epic offers in its state, each with its availability and the reason when disabled: a plan running, pausing, paused, waiting for the operator (those of running beside the decision), held (those of running and the acts of its hold), ended (chat alone); an epic running, waiting, held, landing (archive disabled until the ledger has landed), ended. The requests of each action; the confirmation of an end (what ends, the pull request, the reason), the cancel of a plan held by an integrity pause being that pause's abort; the plan outcomes `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released`, and `aborted` beside `merged`, `no_change`, `handed_back`, `returned`, and the epic's `terminal_reason` `completed`, `cancelled`, `released`; next plan with `epic.raise-scope` when the scope is full; the archive check's lines, an open finding holding it and the kept findings and watches listed as carried; the cancel of an epic with the choice for its plans; the epic's inbox records of the operator's acts (`paused`, `started`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `revised`, `settings_changed`) and the queue row's statuses `pausing`, `paused`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`.
* A plan's settings (UC-CTL-5): its values per aspect with their origin, the states `plan-settings.open`, `.aspect`, `.value`, `.validate`, `.apply`, a refused value with its reason, the refusal of a change whose new instance version would not contain the plan's position, the new instance version, and the epic told.
* The decomposition: the draft (workstreams, plans with objective, deliverables, surface, dependencies, order), the checks per plan, the changes of a later run, and its states with read-back and chat: the operator's instruction `decomposition.instruct`, the rewrite `decomposition.rewrite`, and `decomposition.read-back` (accept → the checks, instruct again → `decomposition.instruct`, discard → the review).
* The epic as a resource: its header, workstreams and rows with status and what each waits for, sources, landings, registers, its history.
* The holds and their acts: each hold's kind from exactly `stalled`, `condition`, `integrity_pause`, `held`, `landing_failed`, its cause, attempts, and the acts that resolve it, cancel among them for a plan or an epic.
* The pause with its point (now, after the task, after the phase) and the states pausing and paused.
* The chat (the sub-workflow `chat` of `pm-mcp-core`): Chat as an action of a plan in every state that has not ended and of an epic in every state that has not ended, disabled with its reason while the routed role does not qualify; its subject, scope (the whole plan, the whole epic, the decomposition draft, one register item, the whole register), and routed role (`replan`, `decompose`); its states `chat.open`, `chat.turn`, `chat.read-back`, `chat.apply`, `chat.end` with the transcript, the operator's messages kept as they are and never interpreted, each proposed change, and only that, as a read-back with accept and reject, a plan's chat a sub-workflow call within the phase it is called in (its phase from the call stack), the result of an apply (applied, applied at the next task boundary, a check failed with its reason), and the epic told.
* The chat about ended work: Chat as the one action of an ended plan or epic, on its row of ended work and in its view; the archived record as its scope (outcome, reason, history, decisions, re-planning records, events) read from the archive at the project's scope, routed to `replan` or `decompose`; read-only, its one proposal a follow-up plan whose accepted description opens a start entry (`start-contract`); the transcript kept beside the archive entry, outside the archived record. The chat inside a decision and the start chat are represented with this chat's records, by `decision-contract` and `start-contract`.
* The review: an item (id, statement, rules, origin, trust, state open, kept, dropped, or promoted, proposal), the dispositions (kept as a finding, moved to the watches, dropped, promoted), the planning role's draft and rewrite as states of the review, the edit of a drafted plan (`review.instruct-plan`, `review.redraft-plan`, `review.read-back-plan`), its chat, the read-backs with accept, instruct again, and discard.
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: a plan paused now, after the task, and after the phase, a pause withdrawn while pausing, a plan started again, cancelled with its pull request closed, abandoned while paused, cancelled while waiting and while held, aborted from an integrity pause; a plan's setting changed while it runs, refused for its value, and refused as too late; a first decomposition agreed after an edit and a failing check, a later run with changed, new, and dropped plans; each of them in an epic, the epic told; next plan started, none launchable, the scope full and raised, the scope full and left; an epic landing with its archive disabled; an archive check failing on open findings and passing with kept findings and watches carried; an epic cancelled with and without its plans; a findings review with every disposition, an edit read back and discarded, a drafted plan edited and its redraft accepted, a chat on an item, the remaining proposals accepted, a review left and continued; a chat on a running plan with a question only and with a revision accepted that applies at the next task boundary, a chat on a paused plan of an epic (the epic told), a proposal rejected, a revision whose check fails, a chat on an epic staging a plan, a chat in the decomposition changing the draft, a chat ended with a proposal open, Chat disabled while the role does not qualify; a chat about an ended plan with questions only, one about an ended epic, a follow-up plan proposed and rejected; scenario names `control-<case>`.
