== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the client side of the changes of Controlling Work into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]), once the use cases are agreed, and opens that pull request beside its own.
* The actions a plan or an epic offers in its state, each with its availability and the reason when disabled; the requests of each action; the confirmation of an end (what ends, the pull request, the reason); the archive check's lines; the cancel of an epic with the choice for its plans.
* The decomposition: the draft (workstreams, plans with objective, deliverables, surface, dependencies, order), the checks per plan, the changes of a later run, and its states with read-back and chat.
* The epic as a resource: its header, workstreams and rows with status and what each waits for, sources, landings, registers, its history.
* The holds and their acts: each hold's kind, cause, attempts, and the acts that resolve it.
* The pause with its point (now, after the task, after the phase) and the states pausing and paused.
* The chat (the sub-workflow `chat` of `pm-mcp-core`): Chat as an action of a plan in every state that has not ended and of an epic in every state that has not ended, disabled with its reason while the routed role does not qualify; its subject, scope (the whole plan, the whole epic, the decomposition draft, one register item, the whole register), and routed role (`replan`, `decompose`); its states `chat.open`, `chat.turn`, `chat.read-back`, `chat.apply`, `chat.end` with the transcript, each proposed change as a read-back with accept and reject, the result of an apply (applied, applied at the next task boundary, a check failed with its reason), and the epic told.
* The chat about ended work: Chat as the one action of an ended plan or epic, on its row of ended work and in its view; the archived record as its scope (outcome, reason, history, decisions, re-planning records, events) read from the archive at the project's scope, routed to `replan` or `decompose`; read-only, its one proposal a follow-up plan whose accepted description opens a start entry (`start-contract`); the transcript kept with the archive entry. The chat inside a decision and the start chat are represented with this chat's records, by `decision-contract` and `start-contract`.
* The review: an item (id, statement, rules, origin, trust, state, proposal), the dispositions, the planning role's draft and rewrite as states of the review, its chat, the read-back.
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: a plan paused now, after the task, and after the phase, a pause withdrawn while pausing, a plan started again, cancelled with its pull request closed, abandoned while paused; a first decomposition agreed after an edit and a failing check, a later run with changed, new, and dropped plans; each of them in an epic, the epic told; next plan started, none launchable, the scope full; an archive check failing on open findings and passing; an epic cancelled with and without its plans; a findings review with every disposition, an edit read back and discarded, a chat on an item, the remaining proposals accepted, a review left and continued; a chat on a running plan with a question only and with a revision accepted that applies at the next task boundary, a chat on a paused plan of an epic (the epic told), a proposal rejected, a revision whose check fails, a chat on an epic staging a plan, a chat in the decomposition changing the draft, a chat ended with a proposal open, Chat disabled while the role does not qualify; a chat about an ended plan with questions only, one about an ended epic, a follow-up plan proposed and rejected; scenario names `control-<case>`.

== Done When
