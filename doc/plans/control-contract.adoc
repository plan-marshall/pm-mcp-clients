== What

The package implements in `pm-api` and `pm-mock-runtime` the client side of the specification sections listed under _Trace_; the engine side is `pm-mcp-core`'s `plan-and-epic-control`, `epic-decomposition`, `epic-findings-review`, `epic-auto`, and `chat`. A statement of them that the implementation proves wrong is corrected in `plan-marshall-documentation` in the same plan (link:README.adoc#how[How to Work a Package]).

* The actions a plan or an epic offers in its state, each with its availability and the reason when disabled: a plan running, pausing, paused, waiting for the operator (those of running beside the decision), held (those of running and the acts of its hold), ended (chat alone); an epic running, waiting, held, landing (archive disabled until the ledger has landed), ended. The requests of each action; the confirmation of an end (what ends, the pull request, the reason), the cancel of a plan held by an integrity pause being that pause's abort; the plan outcomes `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released`, and `aborted` beside `merged`, `no_change`, `handed_back`, `returned`, and the epic's `terminal_reason` `completed`, `cancelled`, `released`; next plan with `epic.raise-scope` when the scope is full; the archive check's lines, run after the completion review where the epic has not run it yet (a manual epic, `control.archive.review_first`), an open finding holding it and the kept findings and watches listed as carried; the cancel of an epic with the choice for its plans; the epic's inbox records of the operator's acts (`paused`, `started`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `revised`, `settings_changed`) and the queue row's statuses `pausing`, `paused`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`.
* A plan's settings (UC-CTL-5): its values per aspect with their origin, the states `plan-settings.open`, `.aspect`, `.value`, `.validate`, `.apply`, a refused value with its reason, the refusal of a change whose new instance version would not contain the plan's position, a change against an older revision refused with `conflict` and returning to `plan-settings.value` with the current value (as `onboarding-contract` represents it), the new instance version, and the epic told.
* The decomposition: the draft (workstreams, plans with objective, deliverables, surface, dependencies, order), the checks per plan, the changes of a later run, and its states with read-back and chat: the operator's instruction `decomposition.instruct`, the rewrite `decomposition.rewrite`, and `decomposition.read-back` (accept → the checks, instruct again → `decomposition.instruct`, discard → the review).
* The epic as a resource: its header (with its project, `project_id` and `display_name`, and its link, its `auto_start` and `overlap_max_files` in force), workstreams and rows with status and what each waits for (the closed list `dependency`, `lock`, `scope`, `overlap` with a running plan: `waits: overlap with <plan>` with the files in common, `stale`, `held`), sources, landings, registers, its history; its tokens: the sum of its plans and of its own tasks (decompose, landing, review) with the split, per plan and its own one step away, the count of tasks not measured (the sum then a floor, where some tasks are measured), the state `not_measured` with that count where model calls happened and no task is measured (no sum, no floor), no budget and no money.
* The epic's automatic mode: the action _Automatic_ setting `auto_start` to a named value, never a flip, setting the value it has answered as done; the project's default `epic.auto_start` shown on an epic's `start.ready` as the value in force; the epic's inbox record of the switch; the events of its reactions to a plan's end (a landing with its downstream judgment applied or waiting in `decomposition.review`, dependants held after an end without landing, next plans started or, manual, named as launchable, an overlap found between running plans, the completion review run once through).
* The holds and their acts (UC-CTL-4): each hold's kind from exactly `stalled`, `condition`, `integrity_pause`, `held`, `landing_failed`, its cause, attempts, and the acts that resolve it at `…/holds/{hold_id}/{act}`, cancel among them for a plan or an epic; an integrity pause showing the tampered file with its recorded and current size and digest, with _accept_ and cancel (its abort), no diff and no restore; the states `hold.show`, `hold.resolve`, `hold.apply`: after an act the plan, the epic, or the landing goes on, or `hold.show` again with what still holds and its cause; the events `hold.opened`, `hold.changed`, `hold.cleared`.
* The pause with its point (now, after the task, after the phase) and the states pausing and paused.
* The chat (the sub-workflow `chat` of `pm-mcp-core`): Chat as an action of a plan in every state that has not ended and of an epic in every state that has not ended, disabled with its reason while the routed role does not qualify; its subject, scope (the whole plan, the whole epic, the decomposition draft, one register item, the whole register), and routed role (`replan`, `decompose`); its states `chat.open`, `chat.turn`, `chat.read-back`, `chat.apply`, `chat.end` with the transcript, the operator's messages kept as they are and never interpreted, each proposed change, and only that, as a read-back with accept and reject, a plan's chat a sub-workflow call within the phase it is called in (its phase from the call stack), a revision proposed only once the plan has tasks, in Phase 5 or in finalize before the merge (earlier the chat answers only), the result of an apply (applied, applied at the next task boundary, a check failed with its reason), and the epic told.
* The chat about ended work: Chat as the one action of an ended plan or epic, on its row of ended work and in its view; the archived record as its scope (outcome, reason, history, decisions, re-planning records, events) read from the archive at the project's scope, routed to `replan` or `decompose`; read-only, its one proposal a follow-up plan whose accepted description opens a start entry (`start-contract`); the transcript kept beside the archive entry, outside the archived record. The chat inside a decision and the start chat are represented with this chat's records, by `decision-contract` and `start-contract`.
* The review: an item (id, statement, rules, origin, trust, state open, kept, dropped, or promoted, proposal), the dispositions (kept as a finding, moved to the watches, dropped, promoted), the planning role's draft and rewrite as states of the review, the edit of a drafted plan (`review.instruct-plan`, `review.redraft-plan`, `review.read-back-plan`), its chat, the read-backs with accept, instruct again, and discard.
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: a plan paused now, after the task, and after the phase, a pause withdrawn while pausing, a plan started again, cancelled with its pull request closed, abandoned while paused, cancelled while waiting and while held, aborted from an integrity pause and one accepted (its file with recorded and current size and digest); a plan's setting changed while it runs, refused for its value, refused as too late, and refused as `conflict` after a change in another client and applied again; a hold's act that releases the plan and one after which it is still held; a first decomposition agreed after an edit and a failing check, agreed in an automatic epic (its plans started) and in a manual one (left staged, `epic.launchable`), a later run with changed, new, and dropped plans; each of them in an epic, the epic told; next plan started, none launchable, the scope full and raised, the scope full and left; automatic switched on (plans started at once, and nothing launchable with the reason) and off, switched to the value it has; a row waiting for an overlap and started once that plan ended; an overlap found between two running plans; a landing re-ordering staged rows, and one whose re-cut waits for the operator; a plan cancelled and its dependants held; a manual epic naming what can start; an automatic epic through, its completion review reported; an epic landing with its archive disabled; an archive check failing on open findings and passing with kept findings and watches carried; a manual epic through, archived with its completion review run first; an epic cancelled with and without its plans; a findings review with every disposition, an edit read back and discarded, a drafted plan edited and its redraft accepted, a chat on an item, the remaining proposals accepted, a review left and continued; a chat in Phase 3 that answers and proposes no revision, a chat on a running plan with a question only and with a revision accepted that applies at the next task boundary, a chat on a paused plan of an epic (the epic told), a proposal rejected, a revision whose check fails, a chat on an epic staging a plan, a chat in the decomposition changing the draft, a chat ended with a proposal open, Chat disabled while the role does not qualify; a chat about an ended plan with questions only, one about an ended epic, a follow-up plan proposed and rejected; an epic's tokens partly measured (a floor) and not measured at all after model calls (`not measured` with its coverage); scenario names `control-<case>`.
