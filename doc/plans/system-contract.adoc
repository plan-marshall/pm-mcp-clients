== What

The package implements in `pm-api` and `pm-mock-runtime` the client side of the specification sections listed under _Trace_; the engine side is `pm-mcp-core`'s `runtime-control`, `runtime-metrics`, and `cleanup`. A statement of them that the implementation proves wrong is corrected in `plan-marshall-documentation` in the same plan (link:README.adoc#how[How to Work a Package]).

* The system snapshot as a record of `pm-api`, `GET /api/v1/system` with the window (`hour`, `today`, `7d`) and an optional project: the runtime (version, build, started at, uptime, heap and resident memory, CPU, the machine store's size and free space with when they were measured, the socket and the two web listeners side by side, each open or closed on its own: the loopback listener with its address, the network listener with its address and the number of paired devices), the work (plans and epics by the overview's states, tasks running and queued, workers by role, harness, model, and state, jobs by kind with their slots and queue, locks held, a recovering project marked), the models per model and per role (calls; the unified tokens, total, `in`, and `out`, with the raw components input, cache read, cache write, output, reasoning, each `not_reported` or `not_applicable` where it is; errors and retries; calls not measured, a total over them a floor), in tokens and never in money: no cost, price, or currency field exists (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles § Tokens, Not Money]); every figure with its unit and `unavailable` with its reason as a value of its own, never `0`; the links of a figure to what it counts.
* The runtime's lifecycle in the runtime status (`GET /api/v1/status`): its version, its state (`running`, `draining` with who asked it and whether it can be withdrawn, `upgrading` with its step), the version state (the newest version known with its date, when it was checked, a failed check with its reason, whether this installation can be upgraded by the runtime with the reason where not, and the link to the release notes), and the last upgrade's result.
* The release notes, `GET /api/v1/system/release-notes` with a version: the notes of a newer version known and of each in between, as plain text, a read and no operator act; `404` for a version no check made known.
* The operator's acts under `/api/v1/operator/runtime/`: `POST stop` with `when` (`drain`, `now`) and `then` (`exit`, `restart`), `DELETE stop` (`409` once too late, refused for a drain of a signal), `POST upgrade` with the version and `when`, `DELETE upgrade` (`409` once the links are switched), `POST upgrade/check`, the check for a newer version behind the consent `check_updates` with the states `runtime.update-consent` and `runtime.check-update`, refreshing the version state of the status; the states of UC-SYS-1 and UC-SYS-2 with their links.
* The cleanup (link:../design/shared/cleanup.adoc#requests[Cleanup, The Requests]): `GET /api/v1/system/cleanup`, the open job (`cln-NNNN`, its state `open`, `postponed`, `deleting`, `done`, since when it is due, the summary per kind with the space it frees, the details paged per project: plans and epics with title, outcome, end, size, and logs counted, logs of a plan on their own, read reports per project and of the machine, unenrolled projects with what their store holds; the links of its commands) or none with the next check, the durations in force, the kept count, and the residue figures per sweep step with its last run; `POST /api/v1/system/cleanup/{job_id}/delete` with the unchecked items, `.../remind`, `.../ignore`, each answering the decision already taken on a decided job; `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/system/cleanup/kept` (how each item came to be kept, when, by whom; released by leaving it out); the durations `cleanup.plans_after_days`, `cleanup.epics_after_days`, `cleanup.logs_after_days` (with a project's own value), `cleanup.reports_after_days`, `cleanup.unenrolled_after_days`, `cleanup.remind_after_days` (the machine's only) the five durations and the reminder as values of the machine settings, of which only the plans', epics' and logs' durations may also be a project's own value (reports, unenrolled projects and the reminder are machine-only), at least `7` days or `never`, the reminder at least `1` day and never `never`; the system snapshot's cleanup line; the states `cleanup.job`, `cleanup.confirm`, `cleanup.delete`, `cleanup.remind`, `cleanup.keep` of UC-SYS-3 with their links.
* The report kind `system.cleanup_due`, a report of the machine without a project, added to the closed kinds of `report-contract`, and a report's detail of a deleted plan or epic with `deleted` and its date in place of the subject no longer kept.
* The events of `GET /api/v1/events`: `system.metrics` (the figures that changed), `runtime.draining`, `runtime.drain_withdrawn`, `runtime.stopping`, `runtime.upgrading`, `runtime.upgraded`, `runtime.upgrade_failed`; `cleanup.job_opened`, `cleanup.decided`, `cleanup.progress`, `cleanup.done`.
* The consent kind `check_updates` added to the closed enumeration of the kinds, with its title in `common` (link:../design/shared/consents.adoc#kinds[Consents, The Kinds]).
* The texts of the area `system` added to the catalogue of `message-catalogue` (link:../design/shared/system-texts.adoc#texts[System, Texts], link:../design/shared/cleanup-texts.adoc#texts[Cleanup, Texts]), with `report.subject.system_cleanup_due` and `report.detail.deleted` in the area `report`.
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: an idle runtime; one under load with workers folded by role and more than one page of jobs; a figure unavailable (CPU, a harness that reports no tokens, calls not measured with the total a floor, a figure not applicable to a harness); per project with one project recovering; the listeners: loopback open with the network off, both open with paired devices, loopback off with the network open; each window; the figures changing live; a stop by drain with what is left counting down and stopped; a drain withdrawn, and one withdrawn too late; a drain of a signal; a drain past its bound; a stop now; a restart, with the clients reconnecting; the check declined, remembered, and failing; an upgrade by drain and now, withdrawn before the switch, failing before the switch, and rolled back after a start that failed; an installation the runtime cannot upgrade; a client of an older release after an upgrade; two clients stopping at once; scenario names `system-<case>`. For the cleanup: nothing due with the residue figures; a job due with every kind across two projects and an unenrolled project, more than one page; a job deleted whole, with items left out, and with an item that could not be deleted; a delete refused while the runtime stops; a job postponed and coming back; a job ignored; a kept list released one by one and all; a decision sent for a job already decided in the other client; durations changed for the machine and for a project, one below the minimum, and one changed meanwhile; a report's detail of a deleted plan; scenario names `cleanup-<case>`.

== Done When

Every record and event has a round-trip test of its codec, an unavailable figure never decodes as `0`, and the events and states are enumerations a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
