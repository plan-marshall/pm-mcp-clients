== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the client side of the changes The Runtime's Lifecycle and The Runtime's Metrics into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]), once the use cases are agreed, beside what `pm-mcp-core`'s `runtime-control` and `runtime-metrics` write of the runtime's side, and opens that pull request beside its own.
* The system snapshot as a record of `pm-api`, `GET /api/v1/system` with the window (`hour`, `today`, `7d`) and an optional project: the runtime (version, build, started at, uptime, heap and resident memory, CPU, the machine store's size and free space with when they were measured, the socket and the web listener), the work (plans and epics by the overview's states, tasks running and queued, workers by role, harness, model, and state, jobs by kind with their slots and queue, locks held, a recovering project marked), the models per model and per role (calls, the token components and their total, cost in US dollars, errors and retries, calls not measured); every figure with its unit and `unavailable` with its reason as a value of its own, never `0`; the links of a figure to what it counts.
* The runtime's lifecycle in the runtime status (`GET /api/v1/status`): its version, its state (`running`, `draining` with who asked it and whether it can be withdrawn, `upgrading` with its step), the newest version known with when it was checked, the last upgrade's result, and whether this installation can be upgraded by the runtime, with the reason where not.
* The operator's acts under `/api/v1/operator/runtime/`: `POST stop` with `when` (`drain`, `now`) and `then` (`exit`, `restart`), `DELETE stop` (`409` once too late, refused for a drain of a signal), `POST upgrade` with the version and `when`, the check for a newer version behind the consent `check_updates` with the states `runtime.update-consent` and `runtime.check-update`; the states of UC-SYS-1 and UC-SYS-2 with their links.
* The events of `GET /api/v1/events`: `system.metrics` (the figures that changed), `runtime.draining`, `runtime.drain_withdrawn`, `runtime.stopping`, `runtime.upgrading`, `runtime.upgraded`, `runtime.upgrade_failed`.
* The consent kind `check_updates` added to the closed enumeration of the kinds, with its title in `common` (link:../design/shared/consents.adoc#kinds[Consents, The Kinds]).
* The texts of the area `system` added to the catalogue of `message-catalogue` (link:../design/shared/system-texts.adoc#texts[System, Texts]).
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: an idle runtime; one under load with workers folded by role and more than one page of jobs; a figure unavailable (CPU, a model's cost, calls not measured); per project with one project recovering; each window; the figures changing live; a stop by drain with what is left counting down and stopped; a drain withdrawn, and one withdrawn too late; a drain of a signal; a drain past its bound; a stop now; a restart, with the clients reconnecting; the check declined, remembered, and failing; an upgrade by drain and now, withdrawn before the switch, failing before the switch, and rolled back after a start that failed; an installation the runtime cannot upgrade; a client of an older release after an upgrade; two clients stopping at once; scenario names `system-<case>`.

== Done When

The specification of the changes is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; every record and event has a round-trip test of its codec, an unavailable figure never decodes as `0`, and the events and states are enumerations a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
