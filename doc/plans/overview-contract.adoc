== What

The package implements in `pm-api` and `pm-mock-runtime` the specification sections listed under _Trace_. A statement of them that the implementation proves wrong is corrected in `plan-marshall-documentation` in the same plan (link:README.adoc#how[How to Work a Package]).

* *The overview*: the projects with running work (`GET /api/v1/projects`) and one project with its active plans and epics, its open entries, and its running short landings (`GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}`), each with its `needs_operator` summary (the closed kinds in their order, a paused plan no kind; the holds among them exactly `integrity_pause`, `stalled`, `condition`, `held`, `landing_failed`); a plan's title, phase, activity (`running`, `pausing` with its point, `paused`), `created_at`, `phase_entered_at`, task progress, `off_track` with its signal, shown apart from what needs the operator, and its epic; an epic's title, `created_at`, and counts of running and staged plans; titles and names as the stores keep them; running work as the state catalogue defines it.
* *The events the overview follows* on the runtime-wide stream: `project.work_started`, `project.work_ended`, `project.changed`, `scope.started`, `scope.ended`, `phase.changed`, `activity.changed`, `needs_operator.changed`, `condition.raised`, `condition.cleared`, `hold.opened`, `hold.changed`, `hold.cleared`, `epic.row_changed`, `task.off_track`; their payloads and the codec.
* *Ended work*: the paged list of a project's ended plans and epics (`GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/ended`) with outcome, reason, and end time: a plan's outcome `merged`, `no_change`, `handed_back`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released`, or `aborted`, an epic's `terminal_reason` `completed`, `cancelled`, or `released`; a plan or an epic the cleanup deleted left out, a link to it resolving to its tombstone.
* *The scenarios of the mock runtime* (`overview-<case>`): an empty machine, projects with and without work that needs the operator, a project with only a question of its setup, a project with epics and plans without one, a plan ending while it is selected, a paused plan counted in no badge, a worker off track, ended work with every outcome, a lost connection and its `reset`.

== Done When

Every record and event type has a round-trip test of its codec, and the closed lists (`needs_operator` kinds, activities, outcomes, terminal reasons) are enumerations a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
