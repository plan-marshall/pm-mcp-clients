== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the client side of the change link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/03-plans.adoc#change-process-designer[Designing a Process] into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]), once the use case is agreed, beside what `pm-mcp-core`'s `process-designer` writes of the runtime's side, and opens that pull request beside its own.
* The installed units of a project as a record of `pm-api`, `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/workflows`: each unit with its name, its group (project-owned, axis variant, recipe, compiled only), every level that has it (`project`, `machine`, `default`) with its text, revision, and digest, and the level in force.
* The catalogue of building blocks, `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/workflows/catalogue`: primitives with their parameters and outcomes, the roles of the project's effective role set, the reusable sub-workflows with their parameters and exit contract, the compiled units read only; each with the text it inserts and, where a unit's kind cannot use it, the reason.
* The validation, `POST /api/v1/workflows/validate` with the text, the unit's kind and name, and the project it is checked against: the stages (parse, static rules, compiled invariants, composition) each passed, failed, or not run; the findings with their rule, line, column, and message; the parsed structure of the unit wherever the text parses (the designer refreshes its structure from it after a pause in typing, link:../design/web-app/process-designer.adoc#editor[Process Designer, The Text and the Structure]); and, when valid, the composed process in the representation of a plan's process (`process-contract`), every node planned. Nothing is started or written.
* Placing and removing as operator acts of the web channel, audited with channel, device, and digest: `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/workflows/{unit}` (machine level, `<PM_MCP_BASE>/workflows/<unit>.pmwf`) and `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/workflows/{unit}` (project level, `.marshall/config/<unit>.pmwf`, a recipe's `.marshall/recipes/<id>.pmwf`, a short landing; under `landing.files` written and left uncommitted in the operator's checkout); a recipe is refused at machine level with its reason; placing or removing at machine level validates against every enrolled project the unit would reach and is refused while any fails, the findings naming each project; a project-level unit takes effect for a plan when its checkout or worktree has it (new plans once it landed on the base branch, running plans through `config_drift` at their next sync with the base); the request carries the digest of the unit it replaces and is refused with `conflict` and the current unit when it changed meanwhile; placing validates again and is refused with the findings; a compiled-only unit is refused; the result of a project placing as states of its landing (written, committed, landed, failed with its reason); the events of a unit placed, landed, and removed. Download is the client's and has no request.
* The texts of the area `designer` and the key `operator.help.designer` added to the catalogue of `message-catalogue` (link:../design/web-app/process-designer-texts.adoc#texts[Process Designer, Texts]).
* The scenarios of the mock runtime: a project with units at every level and a compiled-only one; the catalogue with a block a unit cannot use; a valid unit with its composed process and the composition leaving phases out; a finding at each stage (a parse error with its line, a static rule, a compiled invariant, a composition that fails against the project) and the stages after it not run; placing on the machine with projects that keep their own copy; placing on the machine refused by a finding in one of the projects it reaches; removing the machine's copy refused the same way; a recipe placed in its project and refused on the machine; placing in a project landed, under `landing.files`, and with a failed landing tried again; a conflict because the unit changed meanwhile; a placing refused by its re-validation; a compiled-only unit refused; removing at each level, falling back to the next; scenario names `designer-<case>`.

== Done When

The specification of the change is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; every record and event has a round-trip test of its codec, and the stages, groups, and levels are enumerations a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
