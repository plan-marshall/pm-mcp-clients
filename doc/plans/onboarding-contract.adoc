== What

The package implements in `pm-api` and `pm-mock-runtime` the client side of the specification sections listed under _Trace_; the engine side is `pm-mcp-core`'s `machine-onboarding-unit`. A statement of them that the implementation proves wrong is corrected in `plan-marshall-documentation` in the same plan (link:README.adoc#how[How to Work a Package]).

* *The onboarding's states and stores*: the machine scope and its onboarding's states (`onboard.entry` to `onboard.completed`, the aspects' sub-workflows) on the scheme of `operator-interaction-contract`; consents (the closed list of kinds, each with whether it can be remembered and what its _no_ does, `consents.json`); languages as far as `message-catalogue` leaves them; the harnesses from one scan with their default (`harness.none` also when every chosen harness fails); the login probe and the unknown harness; the model catalogue; the steward (on the default harness, outside the catalogue); the default and machine role assignment; the credential store; the providers and their verification; the plan axes with the variants `landing.pull-request`, `landing.main`, `landing.files`; the plan overrides; the harness icons.
* *The machine settings* (UC-ONB-10): `settings.choose`, a later run of an aspect with its current values as the proposal, withdrawing a remembered consent (`settings.withdraw`, `settings.remove-consent`), `sources.documents_mode` (`settings.documents-mode`), the cleanup's durations (`settings.cleanup-durations`, their values as `system-contract` represents them), a credential rotated or replaced with its verification (`settings.credential`, `settings.store-credential`, `settings.verify-providers`); the revision every settings change carries and the refusal `conflict` (`409`) of a change against an older revision, returning the current value (one representation, used again by `project-contract` and `control-contract`; link:../design/shared/work-actions.adoc#conflict[Changed in Two Clients at Once]).
* *Tokens, not money*: the consent field `tokens` in place of a cost, an estimate in tokens with the number of earlier runs, or not known yet, never money (the kind `model_cost` keeps its name); each harness of the scan, the enrolment, and the machine settings with the usage report of this harness and version (`complete`, `partial` with the figures missing, `not_reported`; per figure `reported`, `not_applicable`, `not_reported`), from its profile and its verification; the verification's stage `usage` with its result.
* *Automatic onboarding*: `auto.consent`, the closed list of reasons and the item schema of `auto.result`.
* *The scenarios of the mock runtime* (`onboarding-<case>`): every case the use cases name, among them each consent answered no, each check failing, every chosen harness failing, an unknown harness, the automatic run; `onboarding-settings-conflict` (the documents mode changed in two clients, the second refused and applied again); a harness complete, partial, and not reporting, and the stage `usage` failing on a figure the draft names (`onboarding-harness-tokens-<case>`).

== Done When

Every record and event type has a round-trip test of its codec, and every closed list (consent kinds, landing variants, usage report values, the reasons of `auto.result`) is an enumeration a test compares with the specification; every scenario loads and is served by the mock runtime, and a test per scenario asserts the representation a client receives.
