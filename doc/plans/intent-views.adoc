== What

The engine records a plan's intent and outline as structured records and checks them, and an epic states its intent
before it is cut. The clients show none of it yet. This package designs it first (the design documents, `DRAFT`
until the operator agrees), then builds it in both clients.

* *The plan's intent record* in the plan view: the goal, the criteria in EARS form each with the deliverables that
  satisfy it, the non-goals, the operator's decisions apart from the model's own calls (and the builder check's),
  the open questions, the deferred items; after the self-review each criterion met, not met, or not verified with its
  reason; the builder check's open questions; a round of clarification answered with every recommendation, marked.
* *The outline record*: deliverables with their affected files and intents, the criteria they satisfy, the justified
  added dependencies, interfaces and key links where declared; a missing key link from the verification sweep.
* *The epic's intent* in the epic view and in the decomposition's review: destination, done-when checks (after the
  completion review each met or not, with its evidence), non-goals, what is not yet specified, each plan's
  done-when; editing them in the review like a plan.
* *The spread of `over_size`*: where a role's `over_size` multiple is changed, the distribution of the tokens per plan
  task against its predicted size, with the share of past tasks the new multiple would have flagged.
* *Not in the clients*: the records are rendered by the server for the pull request; the clients show the records
  through the local API, never the rendered Markdown.

== Done When

The design documents named under _Where_ show every element of _What_ for both clients, agreed by the operator;
then both clients show them against the mock runtime's scenarios of a plan with an intent record, an outline record,
a self-review with a `not_verified` criterion, and an epic with its intent through its completion review.
