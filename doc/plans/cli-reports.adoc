== What

* `reports [--project] [--older]`, `reports read <report_id>...|--all` (the ids of the last listing), `reports open <report_id>` (the command of its detail, reading it), and `plan report <plan_id>` with `--per-phase`, as operator verbs in the console and as one shell command; an already read report and an unknown id answered as the design says.
* The line on start with the count of unread reports, also after a reconnect; a report arriving as the quiet line above the prompt, coalesced, without the bell or a desktop notification, held during a dialogue and summed up when the full-screen view is left; no badge.
* The full-screen view: `R` from the overview with the count in its footer, the level of unread reports grouped by project, epic, and plan, `Enter` to the detail (reading it), `m`, `M`, `p`, `e`, `?`, live; `c` on an ended plan's view for its completion report.
* Texts from the catalogue only: the area `report` and the terminal's `operator.reports.*`.
