== What

* The actions of a plan and an epic on their rows and in the plan view's head, by state (a waiting and a held plan, a landing epic too); the dialogues of pausing a plan (with its point), of ending a plan (abort from an integrity pause too), of a plan's settings with their refusals and a value changed meanwhile (`conflict`, applied again or kept; UC-CTL-5), of raising an epic's scope, the epic's toggle _Automatic_, of decomposing again, the archive check, and cancelling an epic; the review of findings or watches with its list, its item, keep as a finding or to the watches, the edit of a drafted plan, read-back, and chat; the chat panel of `web-start` opened from a plan's and an epic's actions and from the review, beside the view it opens from and full screen at phone width; the chat about ended work, _Chat_ alone on a row of ended work and in an ended plan's or epic's view, its follow-up plan opening the start at its sources with the description stored; at desktop and phone width, light and dark.
* the epic's view (UC-PLN-5: the path project › epic with the project a link one step away, its mode in the head, a row waiting for an overlap, its reactions among the events, workstreams and plans with their status, sources, landings, registers, the epic's tokens with their split per plan and its own (a floor where tasks were not measured, never money), events); what holds a plan, the epic, or a landing, with the acts that resolve it, and after an act the affected plan, epic, or landing going on or the hold shown again with what still holds (UC-CTL-4).
