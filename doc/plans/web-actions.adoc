== What

* The actions of a plan and an epic on their rows and in the plan view's head; the dialogues of pausing a plan (with its point), of ending a plan, of decomposing again, the archive check, and cancelling an epic; the review of findings or watches with its list, its item, read-back, and chat; the chat panel of `web-start` opened from a plan's and an epic's actions and from the review, beside the view it opens from and full screen at phone width; the chat about ended work, _Chat_ alone on a row of ended work and in an ended plan's or epic's view, its follow-up plan opening the start at its sources with the description stored; at desktop and phone width, light and dark.
* the epic's view (UC-PLN-5: workstreams and plans with their status, sources, landings, registers, events); what holds a plan, the epic, or a landing, with the acts that resolve it (UC-CTL-4).
