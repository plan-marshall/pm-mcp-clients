== What

* The actions of a plan and an epic on their rows and in the plan view's head; the dialogues of pausing a plan (with its point), of ending a plan, of decomposing again, the archive check, and cancelling an epic; the review of findings or watches with its list, its item, read-back, and talk; at desktop and phone width, light and dark.
