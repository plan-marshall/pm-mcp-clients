== What

Two views, each with one job, in both clients.

* *Design first*: the package writes the two views into the shared design and has the operator accept them before building them. Two views were decided against one combined entry.
* *What waits*: the list of `GET /api/v1/machine/needs-operator` in the order served, each item with what it is, where it belongs, since when, what it blocks, and the act that resolves it. Web: a route of its own, a destination of the rail with its count. Terminal: one command, and the badges gain `credential` and `cleanup_due` with their notices and commands (link:../design/terminal-client/04-console.adoc#pushed[The Console, What the Runtime Pushes]). A credential and a cleanup that is due are not items of any plan, so the web app's other counts are unchanged.
* *Find*: `GET /api/v1/find` behind the search button of the top bar, disabled until now (`web.frame.search.pending`), and one terminal command. Matches by kind, running work first, ended work marked; no match says so and keeps the text.
* Texts from the message catalogue only; a title is plain text.
