The operator decided the points and the documents are merged: core #607, clients #570 (`20f971fe`, the design of the clients), docs #201, plan-marshall-mcp #130. The code was not touched. ## The principle behind the decisions
A mechanism that predicts, infers or pre-declares something the process later observes as a fact is replaced by the observation. In the clients this removes what rendered the predictions: the `over_size` distribution, the epic overlap re-check, the re-grounding verdict, and the outline's declared dependencies. It adds the phase `sanity-check`, where the dependencies and markers a plan really added are read from the build and the diff.
