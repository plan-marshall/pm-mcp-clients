## What the clients show (the target text is in the merged design)

- **The hold kind `overdue`**, beside `stalled`. Every list of hold kinds now has six: `stalled`, `overdue`, `condition`, `integrity_hold`, `held`, `landing_failed`.
  - A plan whose CI wait (or an analysis provider's wait) is far past its expected duration is held, while the wait continues and its route is unchanged.
  - Its cause is `no_change` (the checks have not changed for a while; the hold clears on the next observed change) or `long_running` (the checks keep changing but the wait has lasted far too long; the hold stays until the wait ends).
  - Its acts are _Check again now_ (`observe_now`) and _Cancel the CI run…_ (`cancel_run`, confirmed first), beside _End the plan…_.
  - Design: `design/shared/work-actions.adoc#holds`.
  - Texts: `design/shared/work-actions-review-texts.adoc`: `control.hold.kind.overdue`, `control.hold.awaits.ci` (selected on the cause), `control.hold.act.observe_now`, `control.hold.act.cancel_run`, `control.hold.overdue.changed`.
  - Terminal: `design/terminal-client/19-actions.adoc` (the prompt row), `22-texts-actions.adoc` (keys `operator.actions.key.observe_now` c/p, `operator.actions.key.cancel_run` i/b), `04-console.adoc` (kind row, badge `operator.console.badge.overdue`, notice `operator.console.notice.overdue` selected on the cause), `15-overview.adoc`.
  - Overview and needs list: `design/shared/plan-overview.adoc` (marker `! overdue`, `overview.needs.overdue`), `design/shared/needs-and-find.adoc`.
  - Web app: `design/web-app/components-tags.adoc` (attention tone, ReasonBadge text).
- **What a hold awaits**: below its cause, the hold names what it waits for, as the hold record gives it: the kind and the id of the job, task, wait or lease; since when; the bound or expected duration; and the last observed change (`work-actions.adoc#holds`).
- **Counters against their bounds**: `design/shared/plan-view.adoc#bounds` ("Against Its Bound").
  - Each counter as "n of m": retries, the counters of the remediation policies, watch restarts, task re-offers, outline loop-backs, the re-planning budget.
  - A running job's elapsed time against its budget (`running 6 min of 30 min`).
  - The CI wait against its expected duration (`32 min · expected 15 min`).
  - All of it is shown to the operator only.
- **The plan's timeline**: `design/shared/plan-view.adoc#timeline`, reading `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/timeline` (docs `runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#timeline-resource`).
  - In the web app it is a head link _Timeline_, not a fourth tab.
  - Beneath a hold, "What happened before" leads to it.
  - CLI: `pm-operator plan timeline <plan_id>` (`specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc`, `design/terminal-client/01-commands.adoc`), in the closed list of reads.
- **Task events**: `task.offered` and `task.ended` carry `attempt` and `of`, the re-offer budget (`specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc`).
- **Mock runtime**: scenarios `plan-overdue` and `plan-timeline` (`specification/runtime-model/05-mock-runtime.adoc#scenario-coverage`).
