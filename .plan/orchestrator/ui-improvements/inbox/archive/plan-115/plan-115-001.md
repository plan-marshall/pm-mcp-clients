## Change made at the operator's review (in #386 before the merge)
- A subject's chat stays, with its transcript and the role's context, until the operator starts a _New chat_ or the subject ends. Hiding it, reloading, or coming back later or on another device returns to the same chat.
- _New chat_ (`control.chat.new`, `control.chat.new_confirm.*`) replaces _End the chat_ (`control.chat.end`, `control.chat.end_confirm.*`). It ends the chat and opens an empty one on the same subject.
- The earlier transcript stays with the subject for the audit, so there is no _Clear_. Specs unchanged; the wire act stays `chat.end` + `chat.open`.
